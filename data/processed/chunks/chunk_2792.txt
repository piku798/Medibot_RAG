. American ginseng grows wild in the forests of the eastern United States and southeastern Canada. It grows in shady, moist and hilly regions, but the plant is becom- ing increasingly scarce due to over-harvesting and log- ging practices. In Kentucky, Tennessee, Virginia, and Illinois, American ginseng holds status as a threatened or an endangered species. Some botanists believe that pol- lution and a thinning ozone layer are contributing to its decline
