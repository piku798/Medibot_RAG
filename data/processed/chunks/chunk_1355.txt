Precautions When buying epimedium, be sure to pick leaves with a dark color. Those that are yellow or blanched probably sat in the sun too long when drying and won’t be as effective. Also, purchase herbs from reputable companies to ensure their purity. Side effects Ingesting an excess amount of the herb can lead to vomiting, dizziness, thirst, and nosebleed
