Doctors rule out conditions that cause symptoms that resemble epilepsy, including small strokes (transient ischemic attacks, or TIAs), fainting (syncope), pseudoseizures, and sleep at- tacks (narcolepsy). Neuropsychological testing uncovers learning or memory problems. Neuroimaging provides views of brain areas involved in seizure activity. The electroencephalogram (EEG) is the main test used to diagnose epilepsy
