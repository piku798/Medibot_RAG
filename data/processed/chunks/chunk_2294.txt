Superficial fungal infections include tinea capitis, an infection of the neck and scalp; tinea barbae, also called barber’s itch, along the beard area in adult males; tinea corporis on parts of the body, such as the arms, shoulders, or face; tinea cruris, or jock itch, in- volving the groin; tinea pedis, or athlete’s foot; tinea versicolor; and tinea unguium, or infection of the nails. The term tinea gladiatorum is sometimes used to de- scribe ringworm infections in atheletes
