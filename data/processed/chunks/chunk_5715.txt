Ischemia also can occur in the arteries of the brain, where blockages can lead to a stroke. About 80–85% of all strokes are ischemic. Most blockages in the cerebral arteries are due to a blood clot, often in an artery nar- rowed by plaque. Sometimes, a blood clot in the heart or aorta travels to a cerebral artery
