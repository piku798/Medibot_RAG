Some of the short-term symptoms of low-level lead exposure (which is particularly harmful to the young and elderly) include: • decreased appetite • stomachache • sleeplessness • constipation • vomiting • diarrhea • fatigue • irritability • headaches Some of the long-term effects of low-level lead ex- posure include: • learning disabilities • brain damage • loss of IQ points • attention deficit disorder • hyperactive behavior • criminal or antisocial behavior • neurological problems
