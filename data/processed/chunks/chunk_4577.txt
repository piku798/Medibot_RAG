. Treatment Several alternative treatments are effective. Phyto- (plant-derived) hormones The most widely proclaimed remedies in this cate- gory include black cohosh (Cimicifuga racemosa), wild yam (Dioscorea villosa), dong quai (Angelica sinensis), red clover (Trifollium pratense), and licorice (Gly- cyrrhiza glabra). Also in this category are soy and two soy isoflavones—genistein and daidzein. Of all of these, black cohosh, wild yam, and soy have shown the greatest efficacy
