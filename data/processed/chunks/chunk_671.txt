Selec- tive serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) have recently been shown to relieve dizziness in patients who have psy- chiatric symptoms. When other measures have failed, surgery may be suggested to relieve pressure on the inner ear. If the dizziness is not treatable by drugs, surgery, or other means, physical therapy may be used and the patient may be taught coping mechanisms for the problem
