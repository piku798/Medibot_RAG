General use The herb form of gelsemium has historically been used for migraines resulting from excessive cerebral blood flow, severe wheezing attacks of asthma, insomnia, and nerve pain, particularly trigeminal neuralgia. The latter condition is a disorder of the trigeminal nerve, which caus- es shooting pain in the area of the lips, gums, cheek, chin, and occasionally around the eye
