Pranayama, literally the “science of breathing” or “control of life force,” is the yogic practice of breathing correctly and deeply. In addition to breathing, hatha yoga utilizes asanas, or physical postures, to bring about flexibility, balance and strength in the body. Each of these postures has a definite form and precise steps for achieving the desired position and for exiting it
