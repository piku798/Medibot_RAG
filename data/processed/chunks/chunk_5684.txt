. When IBS is suspected, the doctor (a family doctor or a specialist) needs to determine whether the patient’s symptoms satisfy the Rome criteria. The doctor rules out other conditions that resemble IBS, such as Crohn’s dis- ease and ulcerative colitis. These disorders are ruled out by taking a standard medical history, performing a physi- cal examination, and ordering laboratory tests. The pa- tient may be asked to provide a stool sample that can be tested for blood and intestinal parasites
