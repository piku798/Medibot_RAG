. General use The use of epimedium as a medicinal herb dates back thousands of years. Shen Nong’s Canon of Medici- nal Herbs, compiled around 400 A.D., mentions its use. The odorless, bitter herb has been used as a: • Kidney tonic to help relieve problems of frequent urina- tion and correct problems of lightheadedness and weak- ness associated with improper body fluid volumes. • Reproductive system tonic to treat impotence and pre- mature ejaculation
