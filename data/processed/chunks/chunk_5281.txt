Sperm are small cells that carry the father’s genetic material. The sperm are mixed into a fluid called semen, which is discharged from the penis during sexual inter- course. The whiplike tail of the sperm allows the sperm to swim up the female reproductive tract, in search of an egg. The ovum is the cell that carries the mother’s genet- ic material
