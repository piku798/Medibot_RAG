. No one knows for sure how many Americans suffer from IBS. Surveys indicate a range of 10-20%, with per- haps as many as 30% of Americans experiencing IBS at some point in their lives. IBS normally makes its first ap- pearance during young adulthood, and in half of all cases, symptoms begin before age 35. Women with IBS outnum- ber men by two to one, for reasons not yet understood. IBS is responsible for more time lost from work and school than any medical problem other than the common cold
