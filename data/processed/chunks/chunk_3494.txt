. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Allergen—A substance that provokes an allergic response. Anaphylaxis—A possibly life-threatening allergic reaction causing increased sensitivity to an aller- gen. It can result in a sharp drop in blood pressure and difficulty breathing. Antigen—A foreign protein to which the body re- acts by making antibodies. Granules—Small packets of reactive chemicals stored within cells
