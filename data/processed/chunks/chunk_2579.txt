Frey, PhD Gastroenteritis Definition Gastroenteritis is a general term for infection or irri- tation of the digestive tract, particularly the stomach and intestine. It is frequently referred to as stomach or in- testinal flu, although the influenza virus does not cause this illness. Major symptoms include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and abdominal cramps
