Be- cause of its low toxicity and its rapid degradation in the environment, jojoba oil does not pose a risk to non-target organisms or the environment; though as an oil, it should not be disposed of in lakes or other bodies of water. Preparations Jojoba oil is prepared by pressing the jojoba seeds to extract the oil, followed by filtration. It is then pasteur- ized to ensure product safety
