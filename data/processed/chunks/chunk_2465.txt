. Hematuria—A condition in which red blood cells are present in the urine. Blood in the urine may be readily visible or small amounts may give the urine a smoky appearance. Hemostatic—A drug or medication that stops bleeding. Gardenia is used as a hemostatic agent in traditional Chinese medicine.
