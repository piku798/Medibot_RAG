The recommended dietary allowances (RDA) of iron as outlined by the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) are as follows: • Children 0–3: 6-10 mg/day • children 4–10: 10 mg/day • adolescent–adult males: 10 mg/day • adolescent–adult females: 10-15 mg/day • pregnant females: 30 mg/day • breastfeeding females: 15 mg/day A number of herbal remedies contain iron, and can be useful as a natural supplement
