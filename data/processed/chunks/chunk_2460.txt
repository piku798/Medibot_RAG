Preparations The kernel within the gardenia berry is often re- moved for use in herbal poultices put on sports injuries such as sprains, pulled muscles, or inflammation of nerves. The use of gardenia poultices is particularly common in Chinese medicine. Traditional Chinese prac- titioners make a paste of the herb with flour and wine.
