Wells Diabetes mellitus Definition Diabetes mellitus is a condition in which the pan- creas no longer produces enough insulin or when cells stop responding to the insulin that is produced, so that glucose in the blood cannot be absorbed into the cells of the body. Symptoms include frequent urination, tired- ness, excessive thirst, and hunger
