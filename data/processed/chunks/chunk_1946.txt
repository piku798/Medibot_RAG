GALE ENCYCLOPEDIA OF ALTERNATIVE MEDICINE 2 761 Fish oil Because fish oil can thin the blood, it should not be taken together with aspirin and other nonsteroidal anti- inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs, or over-the-counter pain killers), Coumadin (warfarin), or other anti-clotting med- ications. Fish oil does not seem to cause problems with bleeding when it is taken by itself, however. Side effects Fish oil generally appears to be safe when taken as a dietary supplement
