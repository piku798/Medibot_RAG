Diagnosis Doctors test for epididymitis through: • Urinalysis, which will likely show an elevated white blood-cell count and the presence of bacteria. • Urine culture, to identify the organism responsible for the infection. • Examination of discharges from the urethra and prostate gland. • Blood tests to measure white-cell counts, which will be elevated. • Ultrasound, which will reveal an enlarged epididymis
