. The eucalyptus tree is also known in Australia as the blue gum tree or malee. Other names for eucalyptus include Australian fever tree and stringy bark tree. The name is actually derived from the Greek word “eucalyptos,” which means “well covered,” and refers to the cuplike membrane that covers the bud- ding flowers of the tree. The bluish green leaves carry the medicinal proper- ties of the tree and grow to a length of 6-12 inches (15- 30 cm)
