. The liver also collects chemicals from the blood for pro- cessing. Many of these outward bound chemicals are ex- creted into the bile. One particular substance, bilirubin, is yellow. Bilirubin is a product of the breakdown of hemo- globin, which is the protein inside red blood cells. If bilirubin cannot leave the body, it accumulates and dis- colors other tissues. The normal total level of bilirubin in blood serum is between 0.2 mg/dL and 1.2 mg/dL
