. Nutritionists often recommend changes in the diet for patients with inflammatory bowel disease. Food al- lergies and certain kinds of food are linked with the in- creased incidence of the disease. Eliminating diary and wheat products, common allergens, often alleviates symptoms. The incidence of Crohn’s disease is increas- ing in areas where people consume a diet high in refined sugars and carbohydrates and saturated fats and low in dietary fiber
