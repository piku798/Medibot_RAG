The name “grains-of-paradise fruit” dates from the Mid- dle Ages, and denotes the fact that it was once a highly valued commodity. The west African coast became known as the Grain Coast because grains-of-paradise fruit was traded there. Considered to be spicy, hot, and slightly bitter, the active constituents of grains-of-paradise fruit include es- sential oils such as gingerol, paradol, and shagaol
