. Yoga Several yoga positions are popular as methods to ease menstrual pain. In the “cat stretch” position, the woman rests on her hands and knees, slowly arching the back. The pelvic tilt is another popular yoga position, in which the woman lies with knees bent, and then lifts the pelvis and buttocks.
