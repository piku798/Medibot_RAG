. Preparations The leaves and occasionally the stems of damiana are used medicinally. They are normally harvested while GALE ENCYCLOPEDIA OF ALTERNATIVE MEDICINE 2 587 the plant is in flower and then are dried. Dried leaves turn a yellow-brown color and may be powdered, used in capsules, or steeped in water or alcohol. Damiana is al- ways used internally, never topically. Traditionally damiana has been prepared as a tea or infusion
