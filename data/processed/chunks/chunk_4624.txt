. Estrogen—One of the primary reproductive hor- mones in women and present in men. Three frac- tions of estrogen have been identified: E1 or es- trone is moderately active; E2 or estradiol is the most potent; and, E3 or estriol is the least potent and most often recommended for use in natural hormone replacement therapy, often in an 80% to 10% to10% combination of E3 to E1 and E2
