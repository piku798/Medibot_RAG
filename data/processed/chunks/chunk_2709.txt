. Research & general acceptance Geriatric massage is gaining acceptance in the med- ical community. It is being prescribed to elderly patients to improve blood circulation and relieve arthritic symp- toms. It is sometimes prescribed for Parkinson’s disease patients to help improve mobility. While most patients have to pay for this service, some insurance companies do reimburse prescribed massage treatment. As of 2000, however, Medicare and Medicaid do not pay for this treatment
