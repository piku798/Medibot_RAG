Patients should set the alarm and get up every morning at the same time, no mat- ter how much they have slept, to establish a regular sleep- wake pattern. Naps during the day should be avoided, but if absolutely necessary, than a 30-minute nap early in the afternoon may not interfere with sleep at night.
