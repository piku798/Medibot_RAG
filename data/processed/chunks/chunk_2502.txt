The odor will be present for at least 4–18 hours, sometimes even when so-called odorless garlic pills are used. Interactions Garlic does well when combined with coltsfoot or lobelia for treating asthma and bronchitis. Although onion is not as potent as garlic, it has similar actions, and the two often are combined
