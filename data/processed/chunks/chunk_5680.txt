Characteristically, IBS symptoms rarely occur at night and disrupt the patient’s sleep. In 2002 a research study reported that some children had trouble absorbing certain sugars from some fruit juices, particularly apple and pear juices. When children with IBS went off these juices for one month, 46% saw improvement in their IBS symptoms
