Atherosclerosis—A process in which the walls of the coronary arteries thicken due to the accumu- lation of plaque in the blood vessels. Atheroscle- rosis is the cause of heart disease. Angina—Chest pain that occurs when diseased blood vessels restrict the flow of blood to the heart. Angina is often the first symptom of heart disease
