Dehydration—A condition in which the body lacks the normal level of fluids, potentially impair- ing normal body functions. Electrolyte—An ion, or weakly charged element, that conducts reactions and signals in the body. Examples of electrolytes are sodium and potassium ions. Glucose—A sugar that serves as the body’s prima- ry source of fuel
