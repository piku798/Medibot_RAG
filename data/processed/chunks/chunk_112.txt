Dande- lion held a place in the United States National Formulary from 1888 until 1965, and the dried root of dandelion is listed in the United States Pharmacopoeia (USP). Dandelion may be distinguished from other similar- looking herbs by the hollow, leafless flower stems that contain a bitter milky-white liquid also found in the root and leaves
