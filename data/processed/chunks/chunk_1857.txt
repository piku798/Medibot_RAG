. Syrup: Fresh feverfew leaf can be added to honey, or to a simple sugar syrup. The honey will act as a preserva- tive and mask the bitter taste of the herb. Infusion: Two to three teaspoons of chopped, fresh feverfew leaves are placed in a warmed container. One cup of fresh, nonchlorinated boiled water is added to the herbs and the mixture is covered. The tea is infused for about 15 minutes, then strained
