Lifestyle changes that can alleviate heartburn in- clude: • avoiding drugs known to contribute to heartburn, includ- ing aspirin or other nonsteroidal anti-inflammatories • avoiding clothing that fits tightly around the abdomen • not lying down until the stomach is empty—within about three hours of eating • elevating the head of the bed six to nine inches to pre- vent nighttime heartburn • avoiding strenuous exercise for two to three hours after a meal Resources BOOKS Berkson, Lindsey
