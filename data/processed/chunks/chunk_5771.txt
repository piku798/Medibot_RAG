Coronary artery disease—A narrowing or block- age, due to atherosclerosis, of the arteries that provide oxygen and nutrients to the heart. When blood flow is cut off, the result is a heart attack. Plaque—A deposit of fatty and other substances that accumulate in the lining of the artery wall. Stroke—A sudden decrease or loss of conscious- ness caused by rupture or blockage of a blood vessel by a blood clot or hemorrhage in the brain
