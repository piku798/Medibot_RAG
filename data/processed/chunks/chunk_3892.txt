The most common way of transmitting hepatitis C is when blood containing the virus enters another person’s bloodstream through a break in the skin or the mucosa (inner lining) of the mouth or genitals. HCV may be passed from an infected mother to the infant she is carry- ing (however, the risk of infection from breast milk is very low). It also can be spread through sexual intercourse, es- pecially if one partner is acutely infected at the time
