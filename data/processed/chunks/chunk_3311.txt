The symptoms of trench mouth include sudden onset of illness accompanied by pain, bleeding gums, bad breath, and a grayish mucus that covers the gums. Diagnosis A dental examination and history will be taken. As the disease progresses, a dentist will be able to find hol- lowed pockets near the gums. X rays may be used to re- veal the breakdown of bone
