As the flash comes on, there is a feeling of sudden heat that may produce as lit- tle as a beading of perspiration on the upper lip, or a sud- den and uncontrollable drenching. The sudden feeling of heat may be followed by a cold, clammy sensation as evaporation of perspiration occurs.
