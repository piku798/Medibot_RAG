Starvation, circu- lating infections, certain medications, hepatitis, and cir- rhosis can all cause hepatic jaundice, as can certain hereditary defects of liver chemistry, including Gilbert’s syndrome and Crigler-Najjar syndrome. Post-hepatic jaundice Post-hepatic forms of jaundice include the jaundices caused by failure of soluble bilirubin to reach the in- testines after it has left the liver. These disorders are called obstructive jaundices
