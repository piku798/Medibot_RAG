. Causes & symptoms Knee pain is a symptom of many different diseases and conditions. Short-term knee pain may be the result of excess stress on the knee. Possible causes of knee pain include: • Arthritis. Osteoarthritis (joint degeneration), rheuma- toid arthritis (joint inflammation), and septic arthritis (joint infection) can cause knee pain. • Bursitis. Inflammation of the bursae of the knee can cause knee pain
