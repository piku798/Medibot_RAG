. Many health claims are made on the effect that ganoderma has on the immune system. These claims are based primarily on the presence of high molecular weight polysaccharides and free radical antioxidants in ganoderma extracts. Ganoder- ma also contains the elements potassium (K), magne- sium (Mg), calcium (Ca), and germanium (Ge). Ganoderma is used in Japan and China to treat can- cer and to stimulate the immune system after radiation or chemotherapy
