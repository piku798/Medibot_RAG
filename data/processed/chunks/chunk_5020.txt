. Odle Hyssop Description Hyssop (Hyssopus officinalis) is a member of the Lamiaceae or mint family. This aromatic evergreen, clas- sified by botanists as a sub-shrub, should not be con- fused with several distinct species of plants also called hyssop, including giant hyssop, hedge hyssop, prairie hyssop, or wild hyssop. Hyssop is native to southern Eu- rope and Asia
