Atherosclerosis—A process in which the walls of the coronary arteries thicken due to the accumu- lation of plaque in the blood vessels. Atheroscle- rosis is the cause of coronary artery disease. Coronary arteries—The two arteries that provide blood to the heart. The coronary arteries surround the heart like a crown, coming out of the aorta, arching down over the top of the heart, and divid- ing into two branches
