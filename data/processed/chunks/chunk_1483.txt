Side effects Nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea may occur in rare cases. Applying eucalyptus to the skin may cause a rash in those who are sensitive or allergic to eucalyptus. Interactions Eucalyptus works to detoxify the body. If it is used simultaneously with other drugs, the effects of those drugs may be weakened. Resources BOOKS Fischer-Rizzi, Susanne
