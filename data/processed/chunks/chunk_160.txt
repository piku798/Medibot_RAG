The deglycyrrhizinated product was developed to concentrate the demulcent and healing aspects of licorice, while avoiding excess expo- sure to glycerrhizin and its adverse effects when taken in high doses. General use Deglycyrrhizinated licorice is used to soothe and protect the lining of the stomach and duodenum (upper small intestine)— the common sites of gastric ulcers. Ul- cers in the stomach are known as peptic ulcers, while those in the small intestine are duodenal ulcers
