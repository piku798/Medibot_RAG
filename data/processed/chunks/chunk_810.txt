Herbal therapy An herbalist may recommend one of the following herbal remedies for menstrual pain: • Chasteberry (Vitex agnus-castus) for women who also experience breast pain, irregular periods, and ovarian cysts. • Dong quai (Angelica sinensis) for women with typical menstrual pain. • Licorice (Glycyrrhiza glabra) for abdominal bloating and cramping. • Black cohosh (Cimifuga racemosa) for relief of men- strual pain as well as mood swing and depression
