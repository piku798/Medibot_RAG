Particular care should be taken to maintain the cleanliness of the tea-mak- ing process, to avoid contamination by mold or unhealthy bacteria (a cause of health problems in those drinking poor- quality kombucha). Smoking in the same room as the mix- ture may contaminate it. Mold typically appears as green, pink, or black blotches in the culture, and should be thor- oughly removed and discarded
