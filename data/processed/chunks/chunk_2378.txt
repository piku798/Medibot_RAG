Preparations Gammalinoleic acid is found naturally in fish, ani- mal organs such as liver, and certain plant seed oils. The major sources of GLA are borage oil (18–27% GLA), black currant oil (15–20% GLA), and evening primrose oil (7–14% GLA.) GLA is not available as a pure ex- tract, but only as an ingredient in combination formulas
