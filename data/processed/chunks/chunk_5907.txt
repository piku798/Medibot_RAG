Frey, PhD Jojoba oil Description Jojoba (pronounced ho-ho-ba) oil is a vegetable oil obtained from the crushed bean of the jojoba shrub (Sim- mondsia chinenis). The jojoba shrub is native to the Sono- ran Desert of northwestern Mexico and neighboring re- gions in Arizona and southern California. It grows in dense stands throughout that region. The woody evergreen shrub may reach 15 ft (4.5 m) in height
