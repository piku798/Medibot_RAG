When glutamine is used for medical purposes in a hospital set- ting, it may be administered via an enteral route, or through a tube directly into the intestine. In 2002, the powder cost about 10 cents a gram, while the capsules cost between 12 and 23 cents per gram. Capsules deliver fewer grams of glutamine than the powder and the glutamine in capsules does not absorb as quickly as that in powder
