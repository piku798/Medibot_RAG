. S-ADENOSYL-METHIONINE. In several small studies, S-adenosyl-methionine (SAM, SAMe) was shown to be more effective than placebo and equally effective as tri- cyclic antidepressants in treating depression. The usual dosage is 200 mg to 400 mg twice daily. In 2003, a U.S. Department of Health and Human Services team re- viewed 100 clinical trials on SAMe and concluded that it worked as well as many prescription medications with- out the side effects of stomach upset and decreased sexu- al desire
