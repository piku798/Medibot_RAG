. Dietary changes Inadequate or inappropriate nutritional intake can cause fatigue symptoms. To maintain an adequate energy supply and promote overall physical well-being, individ- uals should eat a balanced diet and observe the following nutritional guidelines: • Drinking plenty of water. Individuals should try to drink 9 to 12 glasses of water a day. Dehydration can reduce blood volume, which leads to feelings of fatigue. • Eating iron-rich foods (i.e., liver, raisins, spinach, apri- cots)
