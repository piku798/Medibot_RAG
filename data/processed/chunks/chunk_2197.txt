. Splinting to immobilize a fracture can be done with or without trac- tion. In emergency situations, splinting is a useful form of fracture management, if medical care is not immediately available. It should be done without causing additional pain and without moving the bone segments. In a clinical environment, plaster of Paris casts are used for immobi- lization. Braces are useful as they often allow movement above and below the fracture site
