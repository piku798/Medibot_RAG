Causes & symptoms Approximately one to four days after infection with the influenza virus, the victim develops an array of symptoms. Symptoms are usually sudden, although the sequence can be quite variable. They include the onset of headache, sore throat, dry cough, and chills, nasal con- gestion, fatigue, malaise, overall achiness and a fever that may run as high as 104°F (40°C)
