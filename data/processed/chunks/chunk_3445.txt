. In folk medicine, hawthorn is used as a heart tonic and treatment, to regulate blood pressure, and as a the shrubs that flower in the spring. A member of the rose family, it has been planted along hedges to deter trespassers since the Middle Ages. Hawthorn grows throughout the world anywhere that is moist. Hawthorn is the common name for Crataegus oxya- cantha or other Crataegus species. There are more than 300 species throughout the world
