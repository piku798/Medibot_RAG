Influenza complications usually arise from bacterial infections of the lower respiratory tract. Signs of a sec- ondary respiratory infection often appear just as the pa- tient seems to be recovering. These signs include high fever, intense chills, chest pains associated with breath- ing, and a productive cough or sinus discharge with thick yellowish-green sputum
