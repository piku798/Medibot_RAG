American angelica (A. atropurpurea) has also been used by some herbalists for menstrual complaints, though the Chinese dong quai is most often used in for- mulas for women. Preparations The medicinal part of the angelica plant is the root. Dong quai root can be prepared as an infusion or decoc- tion, tincture, tablet, or capsule
