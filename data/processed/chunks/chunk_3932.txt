Chinese herbalism is a holistic medical system, meaning that it looks at treating a patient as a whole per- son, looking at the mental and spiritual health as well as the physical health, of the individual. Illness is seen as a disharmony or imbalance among these aspects of the in- dividual. Chinese herbalism has been practiced for over 4,000 years
