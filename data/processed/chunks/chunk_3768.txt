Sources of exposure for some heavy metals • lead: old paint, leaded gasoline, old pipes • mercury: contaminated fish, industrial and agricultural wastes • cadmium: industrial waste, insecticides, old galvanized pipes • arsenic: insecticides and industrial processes, some drinking water Causes & symptoms Symptoms will vary, depending on the nature and quantity of the heavy metal, and whether it was ingested or inhaled
