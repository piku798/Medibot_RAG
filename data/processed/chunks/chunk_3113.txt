Other phytomedicinal uses of grains-of-paradise in- clude as a purgative (strong laxative), galactogogue (to increase production of breastmilk), anthelmintic (an- tiparasitic—it is effective against worms, etc.), and he- mostatic agent (purifies the blood). It has even been found to be effective against the dreaded schistosomia- sis, which is a major problem to the medical authorities on the African continent
