Dry extracts of the leaf, standardized to a potency of 24% flavone glycosides and 6% terpenes, are commercially available. A standard dose is 40 mg, three times daily, though dosages as high as 240 mg daily are sometimes indicated. Ginkgo extracts are widely used in Europe where they are sold in prescription form or over the counter as an approved drug
