These occur when increased amounts of uric acid circu- late in the bloodstream. When the uric acid content be- comes very high, it can no longer remain dissolved and solid particles of uric acid settle out of the urine. A kid- ney stone is formed when these particles cling to each other within the kidney, slowly forming a solid mass
