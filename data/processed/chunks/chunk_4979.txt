. The thyroid gland influences almost every organ, tis- sue, and cell in the body. It is shaped like a butterfly and located just below the Adam’s apple. The thyroid stores iodine the body gets from food and uses this mineral to create T4 and T3. Low T4 levels can alter weight, appetite, sleep patterns, body temperature, sex drive, and a variety of other physical, mental, and emotional characteristics. There are three types of hypothyroidism
