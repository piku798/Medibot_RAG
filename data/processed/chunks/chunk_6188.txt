Since kelp is such a valuable source of nutrients, it is often recommended as a dietary supple- ment, particularly for people with mineral deficiencies. Origins Different kinds of kelp have been eaten for nutri- tional value for over a thousand years. The Chinese used kelp and other types of seaweed as medicine as far back as 3,000 B.C
