An infectious fever may also rise and fall throughout the day, reaching its peak in the late after- noon or early evening. A low-grade fever that lasts for several weeks is associated with autoimmune diseases such as lupus or with some cancers, particularly leukemia and lymphoma. Diagnosis A fever is usually diagnosed using a thermometer
