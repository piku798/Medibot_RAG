The patient should report any symptoms that accompany the dizziness, such as ringing in the ear or nausea, any recent injury or infec- tion, and any medication taken. The examiner will check the patient’s blood pres- sure, pulse, respiration, and body temperature as well as the ear, nose, and throat. The sense of balance is assessed by moving the individual’s head to various positions or by tilt-table testing
