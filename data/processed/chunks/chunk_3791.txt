Odle Heel spurs Definition A heel spur is a bony projection on the sole (bottom) of the heel bone. This condition may accompany or re- sult from severe cases of inflammation to the structure called plantar fascia. The plantar fascia is a fibrous band of connective tissue on the sole of the foot, extending from the heel to the toes
