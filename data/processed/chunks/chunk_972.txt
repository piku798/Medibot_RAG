Edema Definition Edema is a condition of abnormally large fluid vol- ume in the circulatory system or in tissues between the body’s cells (interstitial spaces). Description Normally the body maintains a balance of fluid in tissues by ensuring that the same amount of water en- tering the body also leaves it
