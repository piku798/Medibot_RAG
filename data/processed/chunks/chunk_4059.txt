As the fibers of the annulus break down, the nucleus will push toward the outside of the annulus and cause the disk to bulge in the direction of the pressure. This condition most frequently occurs in the lumbar region and is also commonly called a herniat- ed nucleus pulposus, prolapsed disk, ruptured disk, or a slipped disk
