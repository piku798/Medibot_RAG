Blood pressure medications (antihypertensives) Blood pressure medications—alpha-adrenergic ago- nist antihypertensives—that inhibit the stress trigger pathways involved in hot flashes, reduce hot flashes 20% to 65%. Drugs and dosages used include: clonidine, at 0.05–0.2 mg/day; lefoxidine, at 0.1 mg/day; and methyl- dopa, at 250 mg three times daily. Side effects of dizzi- ness and dry mouth were reported
