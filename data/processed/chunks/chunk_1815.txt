. . . . . . . . . . . Antidote—Any substance that slows or stops the effects of a homeopathic remedy. Coffee and cam- phor are considered to be particularly powerful antidotes. Law of similars—A principle of homeopathic treatment according to which substances that cause specific symptoms in healthy people are given to sick people with similar symptoms. Modality—A factor or circumstance that makes a patient’s symptoms better or worse
