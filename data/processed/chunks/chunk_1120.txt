Medication Over-the-counter pain relievers such as aspirin, ac- etaminophen (Tylenol), ibuprofen (Motrin, Advil), and naproxen (Aleve, Naprosyn) are useful for mild cramp- ing and menstrual pain. If pain is severe, a doctor may prescribe narcotic medications, although these can be ad- dicting and are rarely used
