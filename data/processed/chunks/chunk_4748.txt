Hyperopia is the condition in which the point of focus of parallel light rays from an object lies behind the retina. This condition exists when the eyeball depth is too short for the curvature of its lens system. There is a connection between the focusing of the lens of the eye (accommodation) and convergence of the eyes (the two eyes turning in to look at a close object)
