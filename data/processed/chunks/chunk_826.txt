. Infections of the middle and outer ears are often associated with earaches. Other causes of an earache may be the obstruction of the ear canal with a foreign object or excessive ear wax, boils in the ear canal, a her- pes zoster infection of the ear, keratosis of the ear, tu- mors, an infection of the mastoid process, “swimmer’s ear,” and the aftermath of surgical procedures. Ear pain can also be caused by a rapid descent from high alti- tudes, during air travel or travel in the mountains
