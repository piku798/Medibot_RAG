A fever requires emergency treatment under the fol- lowing circumstances: • Newborn (three months or younger) with a fever above 100.5°F (38°C). • Infant or child with a fever above 103°F (39.4°C). A very high fever in a small child can trigger seizures (febrile seizures) and therefore should be treated immediately
