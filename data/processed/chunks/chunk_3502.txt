Causes & symptoms Causes A person feels headache pain when specialized nerve endings, known as nociceptors, are stimulated by pressure on or injury to any of the pain-sensitive struc- tures of the head. Most nociceptors in humans are locat- ed in the skin or on the walls of blood vessels and inter- nal organs
