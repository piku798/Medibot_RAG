As listed by Pre- scriptions for Nutritional Healing and other sources, foods containing substances that can prevent the utiliza- tion of iodine when eaten in large quantities include Brussels sprouts, cabbage, cauliflower, kale, millet, mus- tard, peaches, peanuts, pears, pine nuts, soybeans, and turnips. Limiting consumption of these foods may be recommended for persons with an underactive thyroid
