Diabetic retinopathy—The tiny blood vessels to the retina, the tissues that sense light at the back of the eye, are damaged, leading to blurred vision, sudden blindness, or black spots, lines, or flashing light in the field of vision. Glaucoma—A condition in which pressure within the eye causes damage to the optic nerve, which sends visual images to the brain. Hyperglycemia—A condition of having too much glucose or sugar in the blood
