Angiography is the best test for ischemia of any organ. An electrocardiogram (ECG) shows the heart’s activi- ty and may reveal a lack of oxygen. Electrodes covered with conducting jelly are placed on the patient’s chest, arms, and legs. Impulses of the heart’s activity are record- ed on paper
