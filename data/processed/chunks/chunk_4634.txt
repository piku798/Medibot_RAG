. In his 1979 book, Anatomy of an Illness, author Norman Cousins brought the subject of humor therapy to the attention of the medical communi- ty. Cousins, himself a physician, details how he used laughter to help ease his pain while undergoing treat- ment for rheumatoid arthritis of the spine (ankylosing spondylitis).
