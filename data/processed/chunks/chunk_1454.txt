Species used medicinally include E. globulus, which contains up to 70% eucalyptol; E. polybractea, which contains 85% eucalyptol; and E. Smithii. Eucalpy- tus amygdalina and E. dives contain little eucalyptol and are used to separate metallic sulfides from ores in the mining industry
