Medication A combination of drugs may be needed to control some symptoms, but most patients who have epilepsy take one of the following medications: • Dilantin (phenytoin) • Tegretol (carbamazepine) • Barbita (phenobarbital) • Mysoline (primidone) • Depakene (valproic acid, sodium valproate) • Klonopin (clonazepam) • Zarontin (ethosuximide) Dilantin, Tegretol, Barbita, and Mysoline are used to manage or control generalized tonic-clonic and complex partial seizures
