. Dong quai root can be prepared as an infusion or decoc- tion, tincture, tablet, or capsule. It is also available dried, either whole, diced, or sliced. The herb is nontoxic, but recent findings suggest caution in using it over an ex- tended period of time. The dried root may be chewed in quarter inch segments two to three times daily, up to three to four grams per day
