. Interactions The use of digitalis can increase the toxicity of other cardioactive drugs. Hypersensitivity to digitalis, dehydra- tion, or the use of diuretics that cause people to lose fluids and salts may increase the risk of side effects from digoxin. The risk of cardiac arrhythmias is increased when people taking digitalis also take amphetamines or diet pills; medi- cine for asthma or other breathing problems; or medicine for colds, sinus problems, hay fever, or other allergies
