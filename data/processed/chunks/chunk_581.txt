Weight-loss dieters should be wary of the “yo-yo” effect that occurs when numerous attempts are made to reduce weight using high-risk, quick-fix diets. This con- tinued “cycling” between weight loss and weight gain can slow the basal metabolic rate and can sometimes lead to eating disorders. The dieter may become discour- aged and frustrated by this success/failure cycle
