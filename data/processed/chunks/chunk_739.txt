Treatment To treat dry mouth, the use of caffeine-containing beverages, alcoholic beverages, and mouthwashes con- taining alcohol should be minimized. Drinking water and fruit juices will decrease dry mouth problems. Chewing gum and lemon drops can be used to stimulate saliva flow.
