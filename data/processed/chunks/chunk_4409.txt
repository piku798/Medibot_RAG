There are several hundred homeopathic remedies. They are almost always made from natural materials— plant, animal, or mineral substances—that have been treated to form mother tinctures or nonsoluble powders. Liquid extracts are then potentized, or increased in power, by a series of dilutions and succussions, or shak- ings
