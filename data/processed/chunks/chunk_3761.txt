Lower esophageal sphincter (LES)—Muscle at the base of the esophagus that opens to allow food to enter the stomach and closes to prevent reflux back into the esophagus. Manometry—Procedure that measures pressure. In esophageal manometry, a thin, flexible catheter is placed down the esophagus to measure pressure at various points
