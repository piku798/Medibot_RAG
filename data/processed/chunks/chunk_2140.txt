If the ability to breathe is affected, patients may have to be put on a mechanical ventilator to assist their breathing and are fed intravenously until the paralysis passes. People who show any signs of botulism poisoning must receive immediate emergency medical care. Both in- fants and adults suffering from food poisoning by C. botu- linum require hospitalization, often in the intensive care unit
