Frey, PhD Jew’s myrtle see Butcher’s broom Jock itch Definition Also known as tinea cruris, jock itch is a growth of fungus in the warm, moist area of the groin. Description Fungal infections are named for the affected part of the body. Cruris is derived from the Latin word for leg, hence Tinea cruris, for the fungal rash affecting the area where the leg joins the pelvis
