. Expected results Herbal preparations are very effective in treating epi- didymitis. Some sources say that given in medicinal doses, the herbs pulsatilla and podophyllum can treat epi- didymitis with the same results as conventional medicine. Pain may begin to subside within 24 hours of treat- ment, but complete healing may take weeks or even months. Prevention Using condoms and not having sex with anyone who has a sexually transmitted disease (STD) can pre- vent some cases of epididymitis
