A cup of ginger tea contains 250 mg; an 8 oz glass of ginger ale contains 1,000 mg, and a spiced dish contains 500 mg. To prevent motion sickness, German health authorities recommend 2–4 g of powdered ginger daily. Another recommended dose is 250 mg four to six times a day.
