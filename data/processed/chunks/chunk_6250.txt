Prior to this development, some people may notice blood in their urine. Once the stone is in the ureter, however, most peo- ple will experience bouts of very severe pain. The pain is crampy and spasmodic, and is referred to as “colic.” The pain usually begins in the flank region, the area between the lower ribs and the hip bone
