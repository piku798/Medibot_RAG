. Ménière’s disease—A disease of the labyrinth in the ear, characterized by dizziness, hearing loss, ringing in the ears, and nausea. Syncope—Dizziness or brief loss of consciousness resulting from an inadequate flow of oxygenated blood to the brain. Vertigo—Dizziness associated with a sensation of whirling or spinning.
