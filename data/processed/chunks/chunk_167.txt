Aldosterone—A hormone produced by the adren- al gland, instrumental in the regulation of sodium and potassium resorption by the kidney. Demulcent—An oily or sticky substance used to soothe irritation in mucous membranes. Expectorant—A medication that promotes the se- cretion or expulsion of phlegm, mucus, or other matter from the respiratory tract
