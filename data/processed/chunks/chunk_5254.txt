. Infections are initially diagnosed by the patient’s presentation and by a history of the illness or injury and the symptoms. A complete blood count (CBC) is a simple clinical test that can be used to diagnose an infection. Increases in the total WBC count usually indicate a bacterial infec- tion; decreases tend to indicate a viral infection or a very severe infection, both of which may cause the destruc- tion of WBCs faster than they can be produced
