An extractor, fresh produce, and a commitment of time to juice the items are required. A blender isn’t strong enough to juice raw produce, and ex- tractors are priced from about $120–2,000. Juice should be consumed as soon as possible after extraction because when it’s stored, juice loses its nutritional value
