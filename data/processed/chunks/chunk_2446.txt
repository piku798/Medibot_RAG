. Gardenias were originally found only in China and Japan, but today there are over 200 different species of gardenia, mostly hybrid, in existence throughout the world. Gardenias are most prevalent in China, Japan, tropical regions of Southeast Asia and the Pacific is- lands, and South Africa. With proper conditions, garde- nias grow into shrub-like bushes or small trees that can reach 5 ft (1.5 m) in height
