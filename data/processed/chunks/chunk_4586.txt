Vitamins and vitamin E therapy Vitamin E therapy is perhaps one of the most histor- ically recommended therapies in North America for hot flashes, in a dosing range from 400–1,000 International Units (IU) daily. Since the 1940s, vitamin E has been be- lieved to be useful in reducing hot flashes and another common symptoms of estrogen deficiency—vaginal dry- ness.
