With treatment, symptoms normally resolve within two to three days. Abscesses may be resolved with percutaneous (by a needle through the skin) or surgical drainage. Emphyse- matous pyelonephritis may be treated with antibiotics; however, surgical removal of the kidney (nephrectomy) may be necessary
