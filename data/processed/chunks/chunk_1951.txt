. Serotonin, also called 5-hydroxytryptamine or 5- HT, plays an important role in controlling behavior and moods. It influences many normal brain activities and also regulates the activity of other neurotransmitters. Having adequate levels of serotonin instills a feeling of relaxation, calmness, and mild euphoria (extreme happi- ness). Low levels of serotonin, serotonin deficiency syn- drome, leads to depression, anxiety, irritability, insom- nia, and many other problems
