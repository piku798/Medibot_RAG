. Hyperglycemia—A condition of having too much glucose or sugar in the blood. Hypoglycemia—A condition of having too little glucose or sugar in the blood. Insulin—A hormone produced by the pancreas that is needed by cells of the body to use glucose (sugar), the body’s main source of energy. Ketoacidosis—A condition due to starvation or uncontrolled Type I diabetes. Ketones are acid compounds that form in the blood when the body breaks down fats and proteins
