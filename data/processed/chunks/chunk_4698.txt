. Warm or hot compresses are used to treat abscesses and muscle aches. A warm compress is prepared in the same manner as a cold compress, except steaming water is used to wet the cloth instead of cold water. Warm com- presses should be refreshed and reapplied after they cool to room temperature. Essential oils may be added to moist compresses to increase the therapeutic value of the treatment. Pepper- mint, a cooling oil, is especially effective when added to cold compresses
