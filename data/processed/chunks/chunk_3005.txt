. Interactions Goldenseal is often combined with other herbs in preparations. Myrrh gum (Commiphora myrrha) and echinacea (Echinacea augustifolia) extract may be added to goldenseal in salve preparations. Goldenseal combines well with mullein (Verbascum thapus) for earache, and with chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla) and meadowsweet (Filipendula ulmaria) for stomach aches. Combine in infusion with gotu kola (Centella asi- atica) for a brain tonic. Resources BOOKS Balch, James F., M.D
