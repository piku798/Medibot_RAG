Greg Annussek Goldenseal Description Goldenseal (Hydrastis canadensis) is a perennial North American native plant found wild in eastern decid- uous woodlands and damp meadows as far north as Ver- mont and Minnesota, and south to Georgia and Arkansas. This versatile herb is sought for its valuable rootstock and inner twig bark. Goldenseal is a member of the Ranunculaceae, or buttercup family
