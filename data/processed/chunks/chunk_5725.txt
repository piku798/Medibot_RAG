Angina symptoms include: • a tight, squeezing, heavy, burning, or choking pain that is usually beneath the breastbone—the pain may spread to the throat, jaw, or one arm • a feeling of heaviness or tightness that is not painful • a feeling similar to gas or indigestion • attacks brought on by exertion and relieved by rest If the pain or discomfort continues or intensifies, immediate medical help should be sought, ideally within 30 minutes
