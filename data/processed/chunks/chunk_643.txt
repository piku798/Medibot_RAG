. Colonoscopy—Examination of an area of the gas- trointestinal tract by putting a lighted scope, usu- ally bearing a fiber-optic camera, into the rectum, and passing it through the intestine. Colostomy—A procedure performed when a large quantity of intestine is removed. The end piece of the intestine leading to the rectum is closed. Fistula—An abnormal connection formed be- tween two organs which usually have no connec- tion at all
