Complications The major effects of diarrhea are dehydration, malnutri- tion, and weight loss. Signs of dehydration can be hard to notice but include thirst, dry mouth, weakness or lighthead- edness (particularly if worsening on standing), urine darken- ing, or a decrease in urination. Severe dehydration leads to changes in the body’s chemistry and could become life- threatening
