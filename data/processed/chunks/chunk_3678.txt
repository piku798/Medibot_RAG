. Allopathic treatment Heart disease can be treated in many ways. The choice of treatment depends on the patient and the sever- ity of the disease. Treatments include lifestyle changes and drug therapy, coronary artery bypass surgery, and percutaneous transluminal coronary angioplasty, al- though these are not cures.
