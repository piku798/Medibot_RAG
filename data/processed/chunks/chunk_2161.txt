Odle Foxglove Description Foxglove, also called Digitalis purpurea, is a com- mon biennial garden plant that contains digitoxin, digox- in, and other cardiac glycosides. These are chemicals that affect the heart. Digitalis is poisonous; it can be fatal even in small doses. It was the original source of the drug called digitalis.
