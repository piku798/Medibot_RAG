Aromatherapy—The therapeutic use of plant-de- rived, aromatic essential oils to promote physical and psychological well-being. Autonomic nervous system—The part of the ner- vous system that controls so-called involuntary functions such as heart rate, salivary gland secre- tion, respiratory function, and pupil dilation
