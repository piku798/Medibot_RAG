For rapidly growing KS, a standard treatment is sys- temic chemotherapy with a combination of adriamycin, bleomycin, and vincristine (ABV); however, several studies have reported that single-agent treatments may be as effective as combinations. Single-agent treatments that have shown evidence of effectiveness are a liposo- mal form of adriamycin used alone; methotrexate, and trimetrexate.
