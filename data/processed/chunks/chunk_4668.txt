David Helwig Hydrotherapy Definition Hydrotherapy, or water therapy, is the use of water (hot, cold, steam, or ice) to relieve discomfort and pro- mote physical well-being. Origins The therapeutic use of water has a long history. Ruins of an ancient bath were unearthed in Pakistan and date as far back as 4500 B.C. Bathhouses were an essen- tial part of ancient Roman culture
