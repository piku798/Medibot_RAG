. Goldenseal is a member of the Ranunculaceae, or buttercup family. It is a main- stay of Native American medicine, and a popular folk remedy. Goldenseal has multiple uses, both internally and externally. It is sometimes called poor man’s gin- seng. This traditional medicinal herb has been known by many names, including yellow paint root, orange root, eye root, Indian plant, tumeric root, eye balm, jaundice root, yellow puccoon, and ground raspberry
