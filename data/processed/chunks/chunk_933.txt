Chronic or severe cases of it can cause the affected areas to form thick plaques (patches of slightly raised skin), develop serous (watery) exudates, or become infected. The areas of the body that are affected by AD tend to vary with age. Children under five years old most commonly have AD, but it can occur at any age.
