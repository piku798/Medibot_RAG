Hypercorti- solemia refers to high amounts of circulating cortisol and may be a pathological or non-pathological condition. Description Pathological hypercortisolemia, or Cushing’s syn- drome, named after the United States surgeon, Harvey Cushing (1869–1939), may result from a lung cancer, tumor of the pituitary or adrenal glands, or from kidney failure
