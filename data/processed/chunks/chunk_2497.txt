. A garlic suppository can be used to treat vaginal yeast or mild bacterial infections. A clove of fresh garlic should be peeled and slightly crushed or bruised. If crushed garlic irritates the vaginal tissue, an alternative that might lessen the desired antimicrobial effect is to use the whole, un- crushed garlic clove. The clove should be wrapped in a sin- gle layer of cheesecloth and inserted into the vaginal canal overnight for 5-10 days
