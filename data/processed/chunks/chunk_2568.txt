. Gastrodia is first mentioned in the Shennong Bencao Jing, which was compiled around A.D. 100. A later Chi- nese herbalist named Tao Hong placed gastrodia in the category of superior herbs, which meant that it could be taken for long periods of time, and that it could be used to promote longevity as well as to treat illnesses. It was originally called chiqian, which means “red arrow” in Chinese, because its stem is red and arrow-shaped
