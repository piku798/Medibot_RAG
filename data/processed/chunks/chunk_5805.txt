Japanese traditional medicine see Kampo medicine Jaundice Definition Jaundice is a condition in which a person’s skin and the whites of the eyes are discolored yellow due to an in- creased level of bile pigments in the blood resulting from liver disease. Jaundice is sometimes called icterus, from a Greek word for “the condition.” Description In order to understand jaundice, it is useful to know about the role of the liver in producing bile
