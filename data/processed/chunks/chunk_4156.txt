. Side effects Some side effects such as loose bowel movements, excessive gas, or occasional stomach pain have been re- ported from high-fiber diets. However, a 2002 report told of a study that followed more than 1,000 women on varying amounts of fiber intake. Those with higher di- etary fiber consumption did not report expected symp- toms of bloating, gas and stomach upset, so most people can enjoy the benefits of fiber with minimal side effects
