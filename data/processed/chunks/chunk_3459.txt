. Causes & symptoms Of all the causes of allergies, pollen is one of the most widespread. Trees, weeds, and grasses produce pollen in large amounts for seed production. These pol- lens are dispersed by the wind, and many never reach the intended targets. Instead, they are inhaled through the nose and throat. Different plants release their pollen at different times of the year, so the timing of hay fever symptoms varies from person to person, depending on which plants provoke a response
