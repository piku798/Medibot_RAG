An ointment form of devil’s claw can be applied to the skin to treat wounds or scars. Herbal tea and tincture Devil’s claw tea is prepared by pouring 1.25 cups (300 ml) boiling water over 1 tsp (4.5 g) of the herb. The mixture, which is also called an infusion, is steeped for eight hours and then strained
