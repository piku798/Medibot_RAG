Donepezil—A drug commonly prescribed for Alzheimer’s disease that provides temporary im- provement in cognitive functions for some pa- tients with mild-to-moderate forms of the disease. Ginkgo extract—Made from the leaves of the Ginkgo biloba tree, this extract, used in other countries to treat circulatory problems, may im- prove the symptoms of patients with dementia
