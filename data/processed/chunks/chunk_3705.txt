Beta-blocker—A drug that blocks some of the ef- fects of fight-or-flight hormone adrenaline (epi- nephrine and norepinephrine), slowing the heart rate and lowering the blood pressure. Calcium-channel blocker—A drug that blocks the entry of calcium into the muscle cells of small blood vessels (arterioles) and keeps them from narrowing.
