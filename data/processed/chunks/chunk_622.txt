During a rectal exam, a doctor may be able to feel an abnormal mass. Touching this mass may prove painful to the patient. When a practitioner is suspicious of diverticulitis as the cause for the patient’s symptoms, he or she will most likely avoid the types of tests usually used to diagnose gastrointestinal disorders
