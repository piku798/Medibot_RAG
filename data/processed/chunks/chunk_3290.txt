. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Ataxia—Lack of coordination. Caloric testing—Flushing warm and cold water into the ear stimulates the labyrinth and causes vertigo and nystagmus (involuntary movement of the eyes in a horizontal direction) if all the nerve pathways are intact. Endemic—Belonging or native to a particular lo- cality or people. Paresthesia—An abnormal sensation often described as burning, numbness, tingling, or pin pricks
