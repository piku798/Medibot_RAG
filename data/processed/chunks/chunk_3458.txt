. Hay fever can develop at any age, but it shows up most often in childhood through the early 20s. The term “hay fever” is not quite accurate, since the pollen of hay grasses is only one of the many possible allergens involved, and there is no fever. Although an allergy to pollen does not appear to be inherited, the tendency to allergic sensitivity in general may run in families.
