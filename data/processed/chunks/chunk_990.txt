Elder grows in Europe, Asia, North Africa, and the Unit- ed States. Most medicinal elder is obtained from the for- mer Soviet Union, Eastern Europe, and the United King- dom. The Latin word sambucus is thought to be derived from the Greek sambuca, which refers to a stringed musi- cal instrument popular among the Ancient Romans
