Stress erosions may develop suddenly as a result of severe trauma or stresses on the stomach lining. • Atrophic gastritis. This form of gastritis results from chronic gastritis. It is characterized by atrophy, or a de- crease in size and wasting away of the gastric lining. Gastric atrophy is the final stage of chronic gastritis and may be a precursor of gastric cancer
