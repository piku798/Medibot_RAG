. Expected results Jet lag usually lasts 24–48 hours after travel has taken place. In that short time period, the body adjusts to the time change, and with enough rest and daytime expo- sure to sunlight, it returns to normal circadian rhythm. Prevention Eating a high-protein diet that is low in calories be- fore intended travel may help reduce the effects of jet lag. Resources BOOKS Czeisler, Charles A., and Gary S.
