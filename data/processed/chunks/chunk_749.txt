Frey, PhD Dyslexia Definition Dyslexia is a kind of learning disability noted for spatial reversals and shifts and is sometimes described as a neurological disorder. It manifests as difficulties with reading, writing, spelling and sometimes math. Occa- sionally, balance, movement, and rhythm are affected
