It grows in shady areas, mountain areas, fields, roadsides and forests in China, Japan, and the south- ern United States, more so in the latter because when im- ported, its native insects did not tag along. Kudzu was first seen in the United States as an ornamental plant at the 1876 Philadelphia Centennial Exposition
