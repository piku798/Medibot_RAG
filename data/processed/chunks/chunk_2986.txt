. Diuretic—An agent that increases the production of urine. Edema—Abnormal swelling of tissue due to fluid buildup. Edema, which typically occurs in the legs, liver, and lungs, is often a complication of heart or kidney problems. Electrolytes—Substances in the blood, such as sodium and potassium, that help to regulate fluid balance in the body.
