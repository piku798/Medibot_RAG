Precautions Flaxseed products are not known to be harmful when taken in recommended dosages, though it is im- portant to remember that the long-term effects of taking flax-derived remedies (in any amount) have not been studied. Due to lack of sufficient medical study, flaxseed products should be used with caution in children, women who are pregnant or breast-feeding, and people with liver or kidney disease
