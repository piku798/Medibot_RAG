In healthy men with- out impotence, yohimbe in some cases appears to in- crease sexual stamina and prolong erections. The usual dosage of yohimbine (yohimbe extract) to treat erectile dysfunction is 5.4 mg three times a day. It may take three to six weeks for it to take effect. Most commercially available supplements don’t contain enough yohimbe to be effective
