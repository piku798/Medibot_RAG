Allopathic treatment In allopathic treatment—medical practice that com- bats disease with remedies that produce effects different from those produced by the disease—natural or synthetic thyroid hormones are used to restore normal (euthyroid) thyroid hormone levels. Synthroid, or synthetic T4, is easy to take and works for about 80% of patients. In ad- dition, some patients need additional T3. However, physicians have not agreed for many years on adding this therapy
