Preparations If lotions or oils are used, care is taken to ensure their safety on a baby’s delicate skin. The most important consideration is to use vegetable oils rather than mineral oils, which can clog the pores in the skin. The oil that is used should be warmed in the caregiver’s hands before applying it to the baby’s skin
