Clinical studies have demonstrated that ipri- flavone supplementation may: • prevent rapid bone loss immediately following menopause • increase bone density in postmenopausal women by as much as 3% • increase bone mineral density in women with osteo- porosis by up to 6% • decrease the incidence of bone fractures among post- menopausal women • reduce bone pain caused by osteoporosis • increase mobility in women with osteoporosis • stimulate the synthesis and secretion of calcitonin, a hormone
