Both the bark and the fruit are dried in sunlight or with artifi- cial heat, and are either kept whole or ground to a pow- der for storage. A decoction made of 1 or 2 tsp of the dried, pulverized bark or fruit left to simmer for 15 min- utes in 1 cup of water can be either taken internally three times a day or used externally as a lotion
