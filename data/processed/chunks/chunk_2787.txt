Odle Ginseng, American Description American ginseng, scientific name Panax quinque- folius, is a close relative of Korean ginseng (Panax gin- seng), and belongs to the Araliaceae family, which is the same as Siberian ginseng (Eleutherococcus senticosus). It is a perennial herb, distinguished by its dark green leaves and clusters of red berries, that grows wild in east- ern North America
