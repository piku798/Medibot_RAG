Allergen—A foreign substance, such as mites in house dust or animal dander that, when inhaled, causes the airways to narrow and produces symp- toms of asthma. Antibody—A protein, also called immunoglobu- lin, produced by immune system cells to remove antigens (the foreign substances that trigger the immune response)
