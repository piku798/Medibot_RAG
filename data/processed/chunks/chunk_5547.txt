. Syrup of ipecac is now considered the safest drug to treat poison- ing and is often the most effective. There are different types of ipecac preparations that vary greatly in strength. Syrup of ipecac is best for use at home to treat accidental poisoning. Ipecac fluid extract and ipecac tincture should be avoided, as they are much stronger compounds and can be toxic. Ipecacuanha is a homeopathic remedy made from ipecac by a process of dilution and succussion (shaking)
