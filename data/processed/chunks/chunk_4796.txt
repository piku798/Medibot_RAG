When the muscular walls of arteries are re- laxed, or dilated, the pressure of the blood flowing through them is lower than when the artery walls are nar- row, or constricted. Blood pressure is highest when the heart beats to pump blood out into the arteries. When the heart relaxes to fill with blood again, the pressure is at its lowest point. Blood pressure when the heart beats is called systolic pressure
