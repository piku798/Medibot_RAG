. Chinese patent medicines containing fritillaria can be purchased over the Internet; typical prices are $13–$15 for a 4-oz bottle. As medicine, fritillaria is graded into four categories, based on shape and the location in which it was grown: song-pei, lu-pei, ching-pei, and ming-pei. Because the raw bulb is toxic, all medicinal forms are processed. Good quality processed powder is white and has a fine consistency
