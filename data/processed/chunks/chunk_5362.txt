A patient with proven cancer of the colon, or even a patient who shows certain precancerous signs, will need a colectomy (colon removal). When a colectomy is per- formed, a piece of the small intestine (ileum) is pulled through an opening in the abdomen and fashioned surgi- cally to allow attachment of a special bag to catch the body’s waste (feces)
