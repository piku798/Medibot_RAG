Due to its estrogen- like effects, some women use flaxseed oil to ease breast tenderness, alleviate symptoms of premenstrual syn- drome (PMS), and help control menopausal symptoms. Flaxseed oil has also been recommended to treat skin conditions, inflammation, and arthritis. It is usually taken internally for all the purposes mentioned above
