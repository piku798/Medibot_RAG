. Allopathic treatment “Resting the bowel” is a mainstay of treatment, and involves keeping the patient from eating or sometimes even drinking anything by mouth. Therefore, a patient hospitalized for diverticulitis will need to receive fluids through a needle in the vein (intravenous or IV fluids). Antibiotics will also be administered through the IV. In cases of severe bleeding (hemorrhaging), blood transfu- sion may be necessary. Medications that encourage clot- ting may also be required
