. Massage therapists focus on muscular reactions to the herniated disk. Neurological irritation that comes with a herniated disk will often cause excessive muscle spasms in the lower back muscles. These spasms will perpetuate dysfunctional movements in the joints of the spine and may exaggerate compressive forces on the in- tervertebral disk. By relaxing the muscles, massage ther- apists will attempt to manage the symptoms of disk her- niation until proper movement can be restored
