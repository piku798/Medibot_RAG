. Preparations The most important aspects of preparation for con- stitutional prescribing are the taking of a complete pa- tient history and careful patient education. Case-taking Homeopathic case-taking for constitutional pre- scribing is similar to that for acute prescribing, but more in-depth. The initial interview generally takes one to two hours. The practitioner is concerned with recording the totality of the patient’s symptoms and the modalities that influence their severity
