Endometrial implants are most often found on the pelvic organs, including the ovaries, uterus, fallopian tubes, and in the cavity behind the uterus. Occasionally, this tissue grows in such distant parts of the body as the lungs, arms, and kidneys. Ovarian cysts may form around endometrial tissue (endometriomas) and may range from pea to grapefruit size
