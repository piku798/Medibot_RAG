Diagno- sis begins with a thorough physical exam and complete medical history, usually including comments from family members or caregivers. A family history of either Alzheimer’s disease or cerebrovascular disease may pro- vide clues to the cause of symptoms. Simple tests of men- tal function, including word recall, object naming, and number-symbol matching, are used to track changes in the person’s cognitive ability
