In addition, the teeth, tongue, tonsils, salivary glands, and TMJ should be examined for problems that might be causing referred pain to the ears. A culture and sensitivi- ty test should be done if there is any discharge from the ears. X rays or a computed tomography (CT) scan may be required to diagnose the problem.
