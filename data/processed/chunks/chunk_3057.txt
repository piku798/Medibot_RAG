. Interactions Gotu kola should not be mixed with oral diabetes medication or drugs such as Lipitor, Lopid, Mevacor, and Zocor, all of which lower cholesterol. Gotu kola can raise cholesterol. It is also best not to mix gotu kola with alcohol or sedatives. Resources BOOKS Castleman, Michael. The Healing Herbs. Emmaus, PA: Rodale Press, 1991.
