Dietary modifications A healthful balanced diet and lifestyle are important supports of the immune function. Reishi (Ganoderma lu- cidum), shiitake (Lentinus edodes), and maitake (Grifo- la frondosa) mushrooms are renowned for their ability to strengthen the immune system and their antimicrobial properties
