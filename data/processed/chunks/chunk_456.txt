. Diabetic foot ulcers are a problem since the patient does not feel the pain of a blister, cal- lous, or other minor injury. Poor blood circulation in the legs and feet contributes to delayed wound healing. The inability to sense pain along with the complications of delayed wound healing can result in minor injuries, blis- ters, or callouses becoming infected and difficult to treat. Severely infected tissue breaks down and rots, often necessitating amputation of toes, feet, or legs
