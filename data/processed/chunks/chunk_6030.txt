. Diagnosis Diagnosis of JRA is often made on the basis of the child’s collection of symptoms. Laboratory tests often show normal results. Some nonspecific indicators of in- flammation may be elevated, including white blood cell count, erythrocyte sedimentation rate, and a marker called C-reactive protein. As with any chronic disease, anemia may be noted. Children with an extraordinarily early onset of the adult type of rheumatoid arthritis will have a positive test for rheumatoid factor
