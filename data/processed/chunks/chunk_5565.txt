Side effects The following side effects have been associated with the use of syrup of ipecac. • Loose bowel movements. • Diarrhea. • Fast irregular heartbeat. • Inhaling or choking on vomit. • Stomach cramps or pains. • Coughing. • Weakness. • Aching. • Muscle stiffness. • Severe heart problems often occur in cases of ipecac abuse. Because ipecac stays in the body for a long time, damage to the heart frequently occurs in persons who repeatedly take ipecac to induce vomiting. • Seizures
