. Preparations As a protein, glutamine occurs naturally in some foods, including meat, fish, legumes, peanuts, eggs, tofu, and dairy products. It also is highly concentrated in raw cabbage and beets. Cooking can destroy glutamine, par- ticularly in vegetables. Much of a person’s glutamine needs, even when exercising hard, can come from food sources.
