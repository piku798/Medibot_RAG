Pelvic adhesions & endometriosis Pelvic adhesions cause infertility by blocking the fallopian tubes and preventing the sperm from reaching the egg. Pelvic adhesions are fibrous scars. These scars can be the result of past infections, such as pelvic in- flammatory disease, or infections following abortions or prior births
