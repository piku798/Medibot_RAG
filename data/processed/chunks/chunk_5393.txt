Supplemental vitamins are recommended for treat- ing influenza, and include 500–2000 mg vitamin C, 400 IU to 500 IU of vitamin E, 200 micrograms to 300 mi- crograms selenium, and 25,000 IU beta-carotene. Zinc lozenges are helpful, as is supplemental zinc at 25 mg per day for two weeks or more. Traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) uses mixtures of herbs to prevent flu as well as to relieve symptoms once a person has fallen ill
