. Heart attacks are also called myocardial infarctions (MIs). A heart attack occurs when one or more of the coro- nary arteries that supply blood to the heart are complete- ly blocked and blood to the heart muscle is cut off. The blockage is usually caused by atherosclerosis, the build- up of plaque in the artery walls, and/or by a blood clot in a coronary artery.
