The reader should note that the Chinese don’t recognize fo ti as the herb’s proper name; in Chinese markets it should be referred to as he shou wu or as polygonum. Fo ti root usually comes in slices. The older and larg- er the root, the higher quality and more expensive. In addi- tion, dark roots are considered a higher grade than roots that have white streaks in them
