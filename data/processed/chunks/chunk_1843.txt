Autoimmune disease—Condition in which a per- son’s immune system attacks the body’s own cells, causing tissue destruction. Epstein-Barr virus—A common herpes virus that is responsible for causing infectious mononucleo- sis. This virus is problematic in people who have a compromised immune system.
