. Ginger root is not recommended for people with gallstones. Side effects Ginger may cause heartburn. Interactions Ginger can interfere with the digestion of iron- and fat-soluble vitamins. Ginger also interacts with several medications. The herb can inhibit warfarin sodium, which is a blood thinner. Ginger can also interfere with absorption of tetracycline, digoxin, sulfa drugs, and phe- nothiazines. Consumers should check with their health- care provider for drug or other interactions
