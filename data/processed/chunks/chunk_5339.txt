When the diameter of the intestine is quite large and infection is present, the condition is referred to as “toxic megacolon.” Patients with IBD have a significant risk of develop- ing colon cancer. This risk seems to begin around 10 years after diagnosis. The overall risk of developing can- cer seems to be greatest for those patients with the largest extent of intestine involved.
