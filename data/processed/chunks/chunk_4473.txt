Stud- ies are ongoing, but the German Federal Health Agency’s Commission E, established in 1978 to inde- pendently review and evaluate scientific literature and case studies pertaining to herb and plant medications, has approved hops for sleep problems, restlessness, and anxiety. Hops belongs to the same family of herbs as marijuana, and some people claim it produces a mild, relaxed, euphoric feeling when smoked. There is no sci- entific evidence for this claim
