Depending on the degree of blood loss, a patient with IBD may require blood transfusions and fluid re- placement through a needle in the vein (intravenous or IV). Medications that can slow diarrhea must be used with great care, because they may actually cause the de- velopment of toxic megacolon. A patient with toxic megacolon requires close moni- toring and care in the hospital
