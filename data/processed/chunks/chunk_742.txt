. Nutritional therapy Because dry mouth often causes gum disease, pa- tients should take vitamin C and beta-carotene supple- ments as a preventive measure. Acupuncture Acupuncture has been tried since the late 1990s as a treatment for dry mouth caused by cancer treatments. Practitioners at a California clinic that offers acupunc- ture to cancer patients use a total of eight needles, to stimulate three points on each ear and one on each index finger
