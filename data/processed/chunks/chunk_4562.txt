. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Decoction—An herbal tea created by boiling herbs in water. Roots, bark, and seeds are used in decoctions; boiling the herbs brings out their medicinal properties. Diuretic—A substance that promotes urination. Edema—A condition that occurs when fluid accu- mulates in the tissues of the body. Incontinence—The inability to control urination. Infusion—An herbal tea created by steeping herbs in hot water
