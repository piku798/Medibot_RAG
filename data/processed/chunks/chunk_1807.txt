. Frey, PhD Fever Definition A fever is a rise in body temperature to greater than 100°F (37.8°C). Description A healthy person’s body temperature fluctuates be- tween 97°F (36.1°C) and 100°F (37.8°C), with the aver- age being 98.6°F (37°C). The body maintains stability within this range by balancing the heat produced by the metabolism with the heat lost to the environment. The “thermostat” that controls this process is located in the hypothalamus, a small structure located deep within the brain
