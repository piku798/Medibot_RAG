. Allopathic treatment Pain medications and large amounts of intravenous antibiotics are given. Prompt surgical removal of infect- ed and destroyed tissue is required for healing to take place. Gas gangrene is often treated with the antitoxin for clostridium as well. In a number of cases, amputation may have to be used to keep the infection under control.
