Odle Eyebright Description Eyebright (Euphrasia officinalis) is an annual plant that grows wild in meadows, grassy areas, heaths, and pastures of Britain, northern and western Asia, North America, and Europe. It belongs to the Scrophulariaceae plant family, which also includes the foxglove plant. Eyebright grows to a height of 4-8 inches (10-20 cm) and has small white or purple flowers with red spots
