Other phytochemicals in ginkgo include flavonoids, bioflavonoids, proantho- cyanidins, trilactonic diterpenes (including the ginkgolides A, B, C, and M), and bilabolide, a trilactonic sesquiterpene. The therapeutic effects of this herb have not been attributed to a single chemical constituent; rather, the medicinal benefits are due to the synergy be- tween the various chemical constituents.
