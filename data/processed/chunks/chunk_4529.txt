The tree species of red buckeye grows to heights of between 15–20 ft (5–7 m) tall. • California buckeye, or Aesculus californica, is a horse chestnut tree found all along the Pacific coast. General use Horse chestnuts have been used as fodder for feed- ing farm animals, and some Native American peoples have included them in their diet
