. Causes & symptoms The exact cause and mechanism of hot flashes is not well understood. What is recognized is that as estrogen levels are depleted, whether due to surgical, chemical, or natural age-related changes, the area in the brain that regulates several functions including body tempera- ture—the hypothalamus—becomes “confused.” The core body temperature set point is lowered, and the threshold between acceptable and nonacceptable body heat levels is more easily crossed
