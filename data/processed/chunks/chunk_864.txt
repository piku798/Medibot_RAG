Hearing tests, or audiograms, are sometimes used to determine whether hearing loss has occurred because of infection or persistent fluid, and whether the loss is se- vere. A hearing screen for children old enough to de- scribe their own hearing reliably can be performed in schools or at the pediatrician’s office. More accurate testing is done in a soundproof booth by an audiologist
