Establishing set times for meals and bathroom visits may help people with irregular bowel habits, especially for constipated patients. Although a high-fiber diet remains the standard treat- ment for constipated patients, such laxatives as lactulose or sorbitol may be prescribed. Loperamide and cholestyramine are suggested for diarrhea
