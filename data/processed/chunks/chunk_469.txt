Odle Diamond diet Definition The Diamond diet, popularly known as the Fit for Life Program, is a way of eating designed to be em- ployed as a health lifestyle. Developed by Harvey and Marilyn Diamond, it is a set of dietary principles intend- ed to serve as a blueprint for habits that can easily be- come routine, allowing individuals to take control of their health
