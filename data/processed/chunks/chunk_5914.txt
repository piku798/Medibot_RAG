Jojoba oil is liquid at room temperature because of its un- saturated fatty acids. It does not oxidize or become rancid and does not break down under high temperatures and pressures. Jojoba oil can be heated to 370°F (188°C) for 96 hours without exhibiting degradation in general composi- tion and carbon chain length
