Animal products can also provide a source of io- dine, especially if the animals are fed iodine-enriched foods or salt: beef, beef liver, butter, cheddar cheese, cot- tage cheese, cream, eggs, lamb, milk, and pork. Some foods such as breads may contain iodine additives. Another source of dietary iodine is iodized salt. Iodized table salt was introduced in the United States in 1924 and significantly reduced the incidence of iodine defi- ciency
