. Drugs to lower fever (antipyretics) can be given if a pa- tient (particularly a child) is uncomfortable. These in- clude aspirin, acetaminophen (Tylenol), and ibuprofen (Advil). Aspirin, however, should not be given to a child or adolescent with a fever since this drug has been linked to an increased risk of Reye’s syndrome. Sponging a child or infant with tepid (lukewarm) water can also help reduce mild fevers
