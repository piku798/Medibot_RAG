Interactions Horse chestnut’s ability to reduce blood coagula- tion, or clotting, indicates that it should not be given to those with bleeding disorders or who are taking antico- agulant drugs. It is known to add to the action of such blood thinning drugs as warfarin or aspirin. Resources BOOKS Grieve,M., and C.F. Leyel
