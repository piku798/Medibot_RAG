In traditional Chinese medicine, fritillaria is called chuan bei mu which translates as “Shell mother from Sichuan.” English common names include fritillary, ten- drilled fritillary bulb, and Sichuan fritillary bulb. Its pharmaceutical name, used to distinguish it as a medi- cine, is Bulbus Fritillariae Cirrhosae and it is one of more than 500 plants recognized as official drugs in tra- ditional Chinese medicine
