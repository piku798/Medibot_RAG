When there is a good match between the anticipated flu strains and the strains used in the vaccine, the vaccine is 70- 90% effective in people under 65. Because immune re- sponse diminishes somewhat with age, people over 65 may not receive the same level of protection from the vaccine, but even if they do contract the flu, the vaccine diminishes the severity and helps prevent complications
