. During the first century A.D. Dioscorides, a Greek physician who traveled with the Roman legions, pro- duced five medical texts. His herbal text, known as the De Materia Medica, is considered to be among the most influential of all western herbal texts. It became a stan- dard reference for practitioners for the next 1,500 years. This influential book also included information on medi- cinal herbs and treatments that had been used for cen- turies in Indian Ayurvedic medicine
