. Genistein—A plant isoflavone found as genistin in soy that is used to produce ipriflavone. Isoflavones—Compounds, including plant estro- gens, with two phenolic rings, that are found in significant quantities in soybeans and soy foods. Isoflavones belong to the larger category of flavonoids, plant compounds that are often a source of the plant fruit or flower color, and pos- sess anticancer, anti-inflammatory, anti-allergy, and antioxidant qualities.
