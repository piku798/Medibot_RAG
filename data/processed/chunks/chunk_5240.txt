. The mucous membranes that line the passageways of the body secrete mucus, which contains enzymes and chemicals that kill or disable pathogens. The pathogens are then trapped in the mucus and filtered out or swal- lowed. Commensal bacteria also live on the mucous membranes; there they inhibit the spread and multiplica- tion of pathogens just as they do on the skin.
