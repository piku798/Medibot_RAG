. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Amino acids—Organic acids containing nitrogen that are the building blocks of proteins. Carbohydrates—Organic substances, usually from plant sources. They are made up of carbon, hydro- gen, and oxygen, and are the diet’s major source of energy. Chitin—A transparent horny substance found in the outer coverings of shellfish. Chitin is used to make commercial preparations of glucosamine
