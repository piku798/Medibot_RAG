Other forms of preparation include frying ephedra in vinegar or wine to improve its tonic effect on blood cir- culation, and toasting it to an ash so that it may increase its ability to stop bleeding. To treat fever and chills, Chinese herbalists recom- mend combining ephedra with cinnamon twig and other herbs. Coughing and wheezing are remedied with a mix- ture of ephedra and apricot seed, while licorice is added to the herb for stomachaches
