. Pituitary gland—A small oval endocrine gland at- tached to the hypothalamus. The pituitary gland releases TSH, the hormone that activates the thy- roid gland. Thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH)—A hormone secreted by the pituitary gland that controls the re- lease of T4 by the thyroid gland.
