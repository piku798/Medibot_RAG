Bypass surgery is major surgery appropriate for patients with blockages in two or three major coronary arteries or severely narrowed left main coronary arteries, as well as those who have not responded to other treat- ments. About 70% of patients who have bypass surgery experience full relief from angina; about 20% experi- ence partial relief
