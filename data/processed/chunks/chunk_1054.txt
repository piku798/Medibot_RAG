People with a genetic defect (alpha 1-antitrypsin deficiency) are also at risk. The early stages of emphysema may go undiag- nosed, but the main symptoms are breathlessness, blue- ness of the lips and fingernails, and exhaustion. Sufferers of chronic bronchitis and asthma are also at risk
