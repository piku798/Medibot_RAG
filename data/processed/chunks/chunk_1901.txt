Many individuals with fibromyalgia describe the symptoms as similar to the aches and pains of a severe case of the flu. Fibrositis, fibromyalgia, and fibromyositis are names given to a set of symptoms be- lieved to be caused by the same general problem. Description Fibromyalgia is more common than previously thought, with as many as 3-6% of the population affected by the disorder
