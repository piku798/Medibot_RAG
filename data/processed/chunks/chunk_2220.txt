Internal Internal uses of French green clay are more popular in Europe than in North America, although some Ameri- can alternative healers recommend drinking or gargling with solutions of French green clay to cleanse the diges- tive tract, treat nausea or other gastric disorders, ease menstrual cramps, or relieve sore throats.
