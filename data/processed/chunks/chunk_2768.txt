. Ginkgo has remarkable healing virtues that have been recorded as far back as 2800 B.C. in the oldest Chinese materia medica. Ginkgo seeds were traditionally served to guests along with alcohol drinks in Japan. An enzyme present in the ginkgo seed has been shown in clinical re- search to speed up alcohol metabolism in the body, un- derscoring the wisdom of this folk custom. The leaf ex- tract has been used in Asia for thousands of years to treat allergies, asthma, and bronchitis
