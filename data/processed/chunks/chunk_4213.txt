. Acupuncture, hypnotherapy, and guided imagery may be useful tools in treating pain symptoms associated with Hodgkin’s. Acupuncture involves the placement of a series of thin needles into the skin at targeted locations on the body known as acupoints in order to harmonize the energy flow within the human body. In guided imagery, the patient creates pleasant and comfortable mental images that promote relaxation and improve a patient’s ability to cope with discomfort and pain symptoms
