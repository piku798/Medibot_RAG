. Antibiotic—An agent able to kill or interfere with the development of bacteria and other microor- ganisms. Commensal bacteria—Bacteria that live in or on the human body and are in an often beneficial re- lationship with the human host. For example, some bacteria in the digestive tract produce need- ed B vitamins. Pathogens—Microorganisms capable of causing disease. Peristalsis—The smooth muscle contractions that move food, bile, and urine through their respec- tive passageways
