Metabolic syndrome is de- fined by the National Cholesterol Education Program as the presence of any three of the following conditions: • excess weight around the waistline (waist measurement of more than 40 inches for men and more than 35 inch- es for women) • high levels of serum triglycerides (150 mg/dL or high- er) • low levels of HDL, or “good,” cholesterol (below 40 mg/dL for men and below 50 mg/dL for women) • high blood pressure (130/85 mm Hg or higher) • high fasting blood glucose
