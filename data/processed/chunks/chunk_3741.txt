GALE ENCYCLOPEDIA OF ALTERNATIVE MEDICINE 2 927 Heartburn Histamine receptor (H2) blockers, such as famoti- dine (Pepcid), ranitidine (Zantac), nizatidine (Axid), and cimetidine (Tagamet), decrease stomach acid secretion. They relieve heartburn in about 75% of users. However, they take 30 to 45 minutes to act and usually are taken two to four times daily for several weeks. H2 blockers are both over-the-counter (OTC) and prescription medi- cines
