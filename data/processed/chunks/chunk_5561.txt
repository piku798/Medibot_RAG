. Precautions Syrup of ipecac For inducing vomiting in cases of accidental poison- ing, only the syrup form of ipecac should be used. Syrup of ipecac should not be mixed with milk or carbonated drinks as they may prevent vomiting. If syrup of ipecac is not immediately available in the home, it generally can- not be used. A 2002 report studied parents’ attempts to administer the syrup upon calling a poison center when they felt they could obtain it within 15 minutes
