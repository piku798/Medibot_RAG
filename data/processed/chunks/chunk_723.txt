. Infusion or decoction: Research indicates that ex- tracts of dong quai that retain the volatile constituents act to raise blood pressure and relax uterine muscles. An infu- sion of the root, steeped in hot water, retains the volatile constituents and is useful to treat dysmenorrhea and to quiet uterine spasm.
