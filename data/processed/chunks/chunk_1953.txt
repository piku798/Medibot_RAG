. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Essential fatty acid (EFA)—A fatty acid that the body cannot make but must obtain from the diet. EFAs include omega-6 fatty acids found in prim- rose and safflower oils, and omega-3 fatty acids oils found in fatty fish and flaxseed, canola, soy- bean, and walnuts. Prostaglandins—Hormone-like substances that the body produces from essential fatty acids
