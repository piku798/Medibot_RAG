. In the summer of 2002, two investigational drugs were announced to become available in the near future to also treat erectile dysfunction. Vardenafil and tadalafil both helped men who also had such conditions as dia- betes, high blood pressure and benign prostatic hypertro- phy. The drugs are awaiting final FDA approval. Vardenafil and tadalafil belong to the same group of chemical compounds as sildenafil, namely phosphodi- esterase type 5 (PDE-5) inhibitors
