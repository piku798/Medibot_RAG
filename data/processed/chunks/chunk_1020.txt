. Description Acupuncturists begin treatment by diagnosing a pa- tient. Diagnosis is performed with interviews, close ex- aminations (such as of the tongue and pulse diagnosis), and other methods. Acupuncture strives to balance and improve the flow of chi, or life energy, which travels throughout the body in channels called meridians. Ac- cording to traditional Chinese medicine (TCM), illness is caused when chi does not move properly in the body
