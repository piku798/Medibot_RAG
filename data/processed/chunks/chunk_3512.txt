. Symptoms Tension headaches are less severe than other types of primary headache. They rarely last more than a few hours; 82% resolve in less than a day. Patients usually describe the pain of a tension headache as mild to moderate. The doctor will not find anything abnormal in the course of a general physical examination, although he or she may de- tect sore or tense areas (trigger points) in the muscles of the patient’s forehead, neck, or upper shoulder area
