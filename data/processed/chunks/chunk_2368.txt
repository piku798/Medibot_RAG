There is also research data that indicates GLA in combination with other measures may help in treating people with Sjögren’s syndrome— a chronic inflamma- tory disease of the immune system that effects mostly older women. Other animal studies suggest GLA may enhance calcium absorption, helping to reduce calcium loss and osteporosis. Osteoporosis is a disease occurring primari- ly in women after menopause in which the bones be- come very porous, break easily, and heal slowly
