Efforts at protecting wild American ginseng have not been successful, as the demand for it in the Far East makes it a lucrative crop for poachers. It sometimes sells for as much as $600-800 per pound. The majority of American ginseng on the market is now cultivated, although it is a sensitive plant and diffi- cult to farm.
