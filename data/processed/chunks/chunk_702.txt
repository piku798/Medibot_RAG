Chinese herbalists prepare dong quai in combination with other herbs, including astragalus (Astragalus mem- branaceus) as a fatigue tonic, mugwort (Artemesia vul- garis), bai shao (white peony), chai hu (bupleurum root),and rou gui (cinnamon bark) in medicinal formu- las for women. Secondary herbs are used to enhance the action of the primary ingredient or to provide additional properties that work synergistically with the primary in- gredient
