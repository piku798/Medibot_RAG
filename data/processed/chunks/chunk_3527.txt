. • Herbals. Feverfew (Chrysanthemum parthenium) can be used for migraine; goldenseal (Hydrastis canadensis) for sinus headache; valerian (Valeriana of- ficinalis), skullcap (Scutellaria lateriflora), or passion- flower (Passiflora incarnata) for tension headache; and cayenne (in nostrils) for cluster headache. A German remedy made from butterbur root (Petasites hybridus) is now available in the United States under the brand name Petadolex
