Examining the skin with a Wood’s ultraviolet lamp is another easy and con- venient method to determine the presence of a fungus. Culture and sensitivity testing can be used if a more de- finitive diagnosis is required. Systemic fungal infections may be initially diagnosed from blood tests. Confirma- tion is determined by cultures made from sputum, blood, urine, bone marrow, or infected tissue samples
