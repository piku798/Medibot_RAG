A study in India published in 2000 reported the ef- fects of ginger (in combination with other spices includ- ing cumin, fenugreek, and mustard) on pancreatic action in rats. During the eight-week study, the combination of spices in more than a single dose stimulated several di- gestive enzymes in the pancreas.
