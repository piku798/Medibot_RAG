Patience Paradox Gentiana Description Gentiana is a plant extract made from gentians, which are a group of perennial plants belonging to the Gen- tianaceae family. There are about 180 species of gentians worldwide. They have a long history of use in healing both in Asian and Western herbalism. In the West, the com- mon gentian used in healing is Gentiana lutea, or yellow gentian
