. Antigen—Part of an invading microorganism, that causes tissue damage and also stimulates the body’s immune system to produce antibodies. Cirrhosis—The end result of many forms of liver disease; the condition of the liver when its cells have been damaged or destroyed and are re- placed by scar tissue. Jaundice—Yellowing of the skin and whites of the eyes when pigments normally eliminated by the liver collect in high levels in the blood
