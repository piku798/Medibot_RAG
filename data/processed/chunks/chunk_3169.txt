However, this limited focus tended to overlook the additional therapeutic possibili- ties of OPCs and, until the latter part of the twentieth century, distracted scientists from investigating broader uses for OPCs. Aside from pine bark, OPCs are concentrated in grape seeds and skins, wine, green and black teas, beans, and the skins of many fruits. Generally, the more intense the color, the more OPCs in the food, which explains why red wine has a greater health benefit than white wine
