The berberine alkaloid in goldenseal stimulates uterine contractions, and the herb is useful to treat pelvic inflammatory disease (PID). Goldenseal is high in iron, manganese, silicon, and other minerals. Gold- enseal was once considered a good substitute for qui- nine. The herb has been used as a remedy for diphtheria, tonsillitis, chronic catarrh of the intestines, typhoid fever, gonorrhea, leucorrea, and syphilis
