. Origins Perhaps the most famous and widely used system is the Bach flower remedies. This system originated in the 1920s when British physician and bacteriologist, Dr. Ed- ward Bach (1886–1936), noticed that patients with phys- ical complaints often seemed to be suffering from anxi- ety or some kind of negative emotion. He concluded that assessing a patient’s emotional disposition and prescrib- ing an appropriate flower essence could treat the physi- cal illness
