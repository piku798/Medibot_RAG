When kelp is harvested, it is cut, dried, then ground into powder. It is this powder that is encapsulated or pressed into tablets. Kelp is available in bulk form either dried or as a ground powder. It is also sold as granules, capsules, tablets, or tinctures. Granulated or powdered kelp can be added to food as a salt substitute
