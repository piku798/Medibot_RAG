Frostbite is classified by degree of injury (first, sec- ond, third, or fourth), or simply divided into two types, superficial (corresponding to first- or second-degree in- jury) and deep (corresponding to third- or fourth-degree injury). Most frostbite injuries affect the feet or hands. The remaining 10% of cases typically involve the ears, nose, cheeks, or penis. Once frostbite sets in, the affected part begins to feel cold and, usually, numb; this is fol- lowed by a feeling of clumsiness
