Other treatments Other remedies for endometriosis include acupunc- ture or acupressure to relieve pain, visualization, guid- ed imagery, naturopathy, homeopathy (Lilium tigrum, sepia, and belladonna), hydrotherapy, exercise, and meditation. Allopathic treatment How endometriosis is treated depends on the woman’s symptoms, her age, the extent of the disease, and her personal preferences. The condition cannot be fully eradicated without surgery
