. . . . . . . Celiac disease—A chronic disease characterized by defective digestion and use of fats. Cystic fibrosis—A genetic disease that causes multiple digestive, excretion, and respiratory com- plications. Among the effects, the pancreas fails to provide secretions needed for the digestion of food. Duodenum—The first part of the small intestine.
