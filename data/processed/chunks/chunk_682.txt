In powdered form, dolomite dissolves readily with effervescence in warm acids. Although rock beds containing dolomite are found throughout the world, the most notable quarries are lo- cated in the Midwestern United States; Ontario, Canada; Switzerland; Pamplona, Spain; and Mexico
