. Other conditions Hibiscus has been credited with a wide range of heal- ing properties. In Colombia, the plant is used to treat hair loss and scurvy; in Samoa, it is commonly given to women who are suffering from menstrual cramps or who are in childbirth, as the leaves ease labor pains. In the Cook Is- lands and the Philippines, the flowers are used to induce abortions.
