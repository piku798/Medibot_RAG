. Barrett’s esophagus or Barrett’s syndrome— Changes in the type of cells lining the esophagus. Sometimes associated with the development of esophageal cancer. Digestive enzymes—Proteins that catalyze the breakdown of large molecules (usually food) into smaller molecules. Endoscopy—Procedure in which a thin flexible scope is placed down the esophagus to examine, biopsy, and/or suture the tissue. Esophagitis—Inflammation of the esophagus
