Causes & symptoms Fatigue may be the result of one or more environ- mental causes such as inadequate rest, improper diet, work and home stressors, or poor physical conditioning, or one symptom of a chronic medical condition or dis- ease process in the body. Heart disease, low blood pres- sure, diabetes, end-stage renal disease, iron-deficiency anemia, narcolepsy, and cancer can cause long-term, ongoing fatigue symptoms
