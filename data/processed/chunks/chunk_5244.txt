The main function of these WBCs, depend- ing on their type, is to engulf pathogens and render them harmless, detoxify poisons, produce and release antibod- ies and chemicals, and clean up wastes left by other WBCs. The spleen, thymus, lymph system, and liver all have roles in the immune response. Successful removal of pathogens from the body often gives immunity against infection by that pathogen in the future.
