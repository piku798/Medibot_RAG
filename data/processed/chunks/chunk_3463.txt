Characteristi- cally, children with hay fever may push their noses up- ward with the palm of their hand or twitch their noses to clear the congestion. Virtually any type of tree or grass may cause hay fever, although plants with showy flowers usually pro- duce a sticky pollen that is much less likely to become airborne
