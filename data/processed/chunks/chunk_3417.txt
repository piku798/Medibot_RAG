Benefits In a celebrated 1990 study, Dr. Dean Ornish’s Pro- gram for Reversing Heart Disease (Random House), a cardiologist showed that yoga and meditation combined with a low-fat diet and group support could significantly reduce the blockage of coronary arteries.
