If there is clutter or disorganization in the section of the room that corresponds to the Lake, or if the room is parti- tioned so that the Lake section is actually occupied by a bookcase or closet, then the environment would be con- sidered to stifle creativity. A feng shui specialist might recommend moving the office to a more hospitable room in the house, or reconstructing the storage space to free up the creative energy in the Lake section of the room
