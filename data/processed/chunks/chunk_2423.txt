. General use Ganoderma is considered one of the most important herbs in Asian healing. Its use extends to almost every system of the body. Not only is it believed to heal physi- cal ailments, it is said to bring about a peaceful state of mind, and to increase spiritual potency energy for Taoists and other Asian spiritual seekers. Ganoderma has been used in China for over 4,000 years. It is the primary shen tonic in Chinese herbalism
