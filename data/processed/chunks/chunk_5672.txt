Drossman in the American Family Physician, an estimated 70% of IBS cases can be described as “mild”; 25% as “moderate”; and 5% as “severe.” In mild cases the symptoms are slight. As a general rule, they are not present all the time and do not interfere with work and other normal activities. Moderate IBS disrupts normal activities and may cause some psychological problems
