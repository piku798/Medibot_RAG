Odle Dong quai Description Dong quai (Angelica sinensis), also called Chinese angelica, is a member of the Umbelliferae (Apiaceae), or carrot family. This Oriental medicinal herb is some- times called the empress of herbs, or female ginseng. Dong quai grows best in such damp places as moist meadows, river banks, and mountain ravines. It may be biennial or perennial
