. Diagnosis A doctor should perform a thorough pelvic exam and take a patient history to rule out any underlying con- dition that could cause cramps. Treatment Nutritional therapy The following dietary changes may help prevent or treat menstrual pain: • Increased dietary intake of foods such as fiber, calcium, soy foods, fruits and vegetables. • Decreased consumption of foods that exacerbate PMS. They include caffeine, salt and sugar. • Quitting smoking. Smoking has been found to worsen cramps
