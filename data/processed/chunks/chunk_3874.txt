. Specific indications The action of Hepar sulph. prevents the formation of pus and hastens healing of abscesses. In fact, Hepar sulph. is one of the best remedies for abscesses, but is useful only before the abscess is open. The Hepar sulph. abscess is swollen and painful, with needle-like pains. Hepar sulph. colds are frequently brought on by ex- posure to cold, dry weather. A cold wind causes sneez- ing and a runny nose
