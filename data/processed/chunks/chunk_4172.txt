Precautions The primary risk to the patient is a mild stinging or burning sensation during the drawing of blood, with minor swelling or bruising afterward. Some patients may feel faint or lightheaded when blood is drawn. Side effects There are generally no side effects associated with the test. Any weakness, fainting, sweating, or other un- usual reaction should be immediately reported
