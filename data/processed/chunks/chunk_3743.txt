Although it appears safe to take PPIs for at least 10 years, the lowest effective dosage reduces the risk of side effects that may include headache, diarrhea, stomach pain, and interactions with other medications. Common PPIs include lansoprazole (Prevacid), omepra- zole (Prilosec), rabeprazole (Aciphex), pantoprazole (Protonix), and esomeprazole (Nexium)
