. . . . . . . Acute prescribing—Homeopathic treatment for self-limiting illnesses with abrupt onset. Allopathy—Conventional medical treatment of disease symptoms that uses substances or tech- niques to oppose or suppress the symptoms. Law of similars—The basic principle of homeo- pathic medicine that governs the selection of a specific remedy. It holds that a substance of natur- al origin that produces certain symptoms in a healthy person will cure those same symptoms in a sick person
