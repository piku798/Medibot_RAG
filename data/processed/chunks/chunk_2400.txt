Dry gangrene is often seen in advanced cases of dia- betes and arteriosclerosis. The tissue doesn’t become in- fected, rather it dries out and shrivels over a period of weeks or months. Wet gangrene progresses much more rapidly. The affected area becomes swollen and gives off a foul smelling discharge. Death may occur within a matter of hours or days
