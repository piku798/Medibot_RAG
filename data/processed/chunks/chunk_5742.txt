. Herbal therapy Western herbal medicine recommends hawthorn (Crataegus laevigata or C. oxyacantha) to help prevent long-term angina, since this herb strengthens heart mus- cles’ ability to contract. Homeopathy Cactus grandiflorus is a homeopathic remedy made from night-blooming cactus and used for pain relief dur- ing an attack.
