. With infant botulism, the spores of C. botulinum lodge in the infant’s intestinal tract. Honey, especially when consumed by infants younger than 12 months, is sometimes the source of these spores. Onset of the symptoms is gradual. The infant initially has constipa- tion, followed by poor feeding, lethargy, weakness, drooling, and a distinctive wailing cry. Eventually the baby loses the ability to control its head muscles. Paraly- sis then progresses to the rest of the body
