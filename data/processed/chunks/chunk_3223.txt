It is one of the primary therapeutic substances used in Ayurvedic medicine to prevent atherosclerosis, as well as one of the most promising herbs or supplements for the prevention and treatment of this condition. Studies in animals have documented not only the protective effects of guggul against atherosclerosis, but have shown actual re- gression of the condition in animals that already had it. The active portion of the plant is the gum resin, which contains guggulsterone, a steroid compound
