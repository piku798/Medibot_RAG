Many health food stores carry fresh or dried eucalyptus leaf in bulk. Eucalyptus can be ingested through the use of teas or tincture prepa- rations, inhaled, or applied externally. Eucalyptus infusion is ingested to treat coughs, colds, bronchitis, congestion, and throat infections
