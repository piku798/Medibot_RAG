Blastocyst—A cluster of cells representing multi- ple cell divisions that have occurred in the fallopi- an tube after successful fertilization of an ovum by a sperm. This is the developmental form that must enter the uterus and implant to achieve pregnan- cy. Cervix—The opening from the vagina leading into the uterus. Embryo—The stage of development of a baby be- tween the second and eighth weeks after concep- tion
