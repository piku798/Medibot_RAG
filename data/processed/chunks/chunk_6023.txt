. JRA is considered by some to be an autoimmune disorder. Autoimmune disorders occur when the body’s immune system mistakenly iden- tifies the body’s own tissue as foreign, and attacks those tissues, as if trying to rid the body of an invader (such as a bacteria, virus, or fungi). While an autoimmune mech- anism is strongly suspected, certain markers of such a mechanism (such as rheumatoid factor, often present in adults with such disorders) are rarely present in children with JRA
