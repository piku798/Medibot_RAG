. Aromatherapy Aromatherapy may be a useful supportive measure in infectious conditions. An essential oil of cedarwood (Cedrus atlantica) is recommended in fungal infections; essential oils of tea tree (Melaleuca alternifolia) and patchouli are also recommended.
