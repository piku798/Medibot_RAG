A general precaution to observe when using any Chinese patent medicine is to purchase only well-known brands recommended by a practitioner of traditional Chi- nese medicine. Cases have been reported of incorrect la- beling, contamination with heavy metals, and substitu- tion of Western pharmaceuticals for the Chinese ingredi- ents. Any of these occurrences can present a serious health hazard
