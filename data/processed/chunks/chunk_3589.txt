. More than 1.5 million Americans suffer a heart attack every year, and almost half a million die, according to the American Heart Association. Most heart attacks are the end result of years of silent but progressive coronary artery disease, which can be prevented in many people. A heart attack is often the first symptom of coronary artery disease. Ac- cording to the American Heart Association, 63% of women and 48% of men who died suddenly of coronary artery disease had no previous symptoms
