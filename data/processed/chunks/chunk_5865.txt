Odle Jet lag Definition Jet lag is a condition marked by fatigue, insomnia, and irritability that is caused by air travel through chang- ing time zones. It is commonplace: a 2002 study of inter- national business travelers (IBTs) found that jet lag was one of the most common health problems reported, af- fecting as many as 74% of IBTs
