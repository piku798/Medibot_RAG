Treatment of food poisoning that is usually not an emergency situation may include drugs such as ipecac syrup to induce vomiting or laxatives to empty the in- testines. Intravenous fluids containing salts and dextrose may be given to correct dehydration and electrolyte im- balances. Pain medications are given for severe stomach pain. Atropine is given for muscarine-type mushroom poisoning
