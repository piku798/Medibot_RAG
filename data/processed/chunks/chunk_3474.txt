Beneficial supplements for treating hay fever in- clude vitamins A, E, and B complex. Vitamin C, espe- cially the buffered type, is a natural antihistamine. In substantial amounts it can help stabilize the mucous membrane response to allergens. Bioflavonoids prevent the release of histamine, and can be taken in combina- tion with vitamin C
