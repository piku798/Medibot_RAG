General use While not yet popular in the United States or ap- proved for use by the United States Food and Drug Ad- ministration (FDA), goldenrod is used widely in Europe to treat urinary tract infections and help eliminate kidney or bladder stones. The Commission E has approved goldenrod as flushing-out therapy for inflammatory dis- eases of the lower urinary tract and for helping to elimi- nate and prevent stones
