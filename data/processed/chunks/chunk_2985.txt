. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Antispasmodic—An agent with the ability to pre- vent or relieve convulsions or muscle spasms. Astringent—An agent that helps to contract tissue and prevent the secretion of internal body fluids such as blood or mucus. Astringents are typically used to treat external wounds or to prevent bleed- ing from the nose or throat. Diuretic—An agent that increases the production of urine
