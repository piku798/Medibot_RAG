In addition, rapid passage of material through the colon can cause diarrhea. Symptoms related to diarrheal illness are often those associated with any injury to the gastrointestinal tract, such as fever, nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain. All or none of these may be present depending on the cause of diarrhea
