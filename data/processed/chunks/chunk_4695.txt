For colds and other conditions with nasal congestion, aromatherapists recommend adding five drops of an es- sential oil that has decongestant properties, such as pep- permint (Mentha piperita) and eucalyptus blue gum (Eucalyptus globulus). Oils that act as expectorants, such as myrtle (Myrtus communis) or rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis), can also be used.
