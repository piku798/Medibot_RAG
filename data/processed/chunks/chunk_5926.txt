. Precautions Jojoba oil is a nontoxic, noncomedogenic (does not clog pores), and hypoallergenic substance. It has been widely used for decades in cosmetics, with no reported ad- verse effects. If jojoba oil is ingested, most of it is eliminat- ed in the feces, with little getting distributed in the body. Side effects No side effects are expected with the use of jojoba oil in recommended amounts, although allergic reactions are a rare possibility
