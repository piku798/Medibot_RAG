Diagnosis When diagnosing the underlying cause of impo- tence, the doctor begins by asking the man a number of questions about when the problem began, whether it only happens with specific sex partners, and whether he ever wakes up with an erection. (Men whose dysfunction oc- curs only with certain partners or who wake up with erections are more likely to have a psychological cause for their impotence.) Sometimes, the man’s sex partner is also interviewed
