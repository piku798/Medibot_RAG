. Polygonum is a member of the Polygonaceae family of plants. In Chinese herbalism, fo ti is called he shou wu or ho shou wu. Other names are fleeceflower and Chinese cornbind. In Japan the herb is called kashuu. It is one of the most popular herbs in Oriental medicine, used as an overall health tonic, as a tincture to increase longevi- ty, and as a remedy for various health conditions. Fo ti is a perennial flowering vine that reaches heights of 3–6 ft (0.97–1.8 m)
