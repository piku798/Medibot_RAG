Culture—A laboratory test in which colonies of microorganisms are grown from tissue samples of an infected individual in order to identify the pathogen. Immunofluorescent assay—A laboratory tech- nique using a fluorescent dye and a special micro- scope to identify the cause of an infection. Meningitis—An infection or inflammation of membranes of the brain and spinal cord
