Another type of elec- trocardiogram, known as the exercise stress test, mea- sures how the heart and blood vessels respond to exer- tion when the patient is exercising on a treadmill or a sta- tionary bike. Both tests can be performed in a physi- cian’s office or outpatient facility. Echocardiography, or cardiac ultrasound, uses sound waves to create an image of the heart’s chambers and valves. A technician applies gel to a hand-held transduc- er, then presses it against the patient’s chest
