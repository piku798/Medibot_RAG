Bilberry (Vaccinium sp.) helps maintain collagen balance and pre- vents the breakdown of vitamin C. Many people with glaucoma have been shown to have deficiencies of chromium and zinc. Supplementation with these two minerals may, therefore, deter the onset or progression of the disease
