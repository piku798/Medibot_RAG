. The cause of hypertension is not known in 90–95% of the people who have it. Hypertension without a known cause is called primary or essential hypertension. When a person has hypertension caused by another medical con- dition, it is called secondary hypertension. Secondary hy- pertension can be caused by a number of different illness- es.
