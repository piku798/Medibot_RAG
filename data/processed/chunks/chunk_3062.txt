Description Uric acid is formed in the bloodstream when the body breaks down waste products, mainly those contain- ing purines. Purines can be produced naturally by the body, and they can be ingested from such high-purine foods as meat. Normally, the kidneys filter uric acid par- ticles out of the blood and excrete it into the urine
