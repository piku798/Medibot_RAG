The GALE ENCYCLOPEDIA OF ALTERNATIVE MEDICINE 2 1092 Ipecac dosage for infants under 6 months old should be pre- scribed by the family doctor or poison control center. For children six months to one year, the usual dose is 5–10 ml or 1–2 tsp. One-half or one full glass (4–8 oz) of water should be taken immediately before or after the dose. The dose may be repeated once after 20–30 min- utes if vomiting does not occur
