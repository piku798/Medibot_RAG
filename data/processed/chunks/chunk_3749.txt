About ten percent of patients with GERD experience esophageal narrowing from acid damage that leads to the formation of scar tissue in the lower esophagus. GERD also can cause laryngitis, bron- chitis, and aspiration pneumonia. After five years of heart- burn, the risk of developing Barrett’s esophagus increases. About five percent of GERD patients have Barrett’s syn- drome. This condition is incurable and may lead to cancer
