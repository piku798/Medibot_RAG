Hatha yoga is the path which has physical health and balance as a primary goal, for its practitioners be- lieve that greater mental and spiritual awareness can be brought about with a healthy and pure body. The origins of hatha yoga have been traced back to the eleventh century A.D. The Sanskrit word ha means “sun” and tha means “moon,” and thus hatha, or literally sun-moon yoga, strives to balance opposing parts of the physical body, the front and back, left and right, top and bottom
