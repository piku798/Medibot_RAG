. A fever is defined as the elevation of the body tem- perature to at least 100°F (37.8°C). Fevers are a helpful part of the body’s response to an infection, since most pathogens do not thrive at higher temperatures while the immune system’s white blood cells (WBCs) work best in a warm environment. If the fever reaches levels of 102°F (38.9°C) or higher, it may have to be brought down to avoid seizures, dehydration, and tissue damage
