. Allopathic treatment Treatment of dizziness is determined by the underly- ing cause. If an individual has a cold or influenza, a few days of bed rest is usually adequate to resolve dizziness. Other causes of dizziness, such as mild vestibular system damage, may resolve without medical treatment. If dizzi- ness continues, drug therapy may be required to treat such underlying illnesses as high blood pressure, arteriosclero- sis, nervous conditions or diabetes
