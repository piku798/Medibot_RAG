To replace fluid that is lost during fever, patients are advised to drink vegetable juices and eat soups. Herbal therapy Western herbalists use tea preparations containing herbs such as bupleurum root or boneset to reduce fever. Mild herbs such as peppermint, elderflower, or yarrow can provide comfort to the child who has a mild fever
