. Eucalyptus oil is obtained through a steam distilla- tion process that removes the oil from the fresh, mature leaves and branch tips of older trees. Approximately 25 species of eucalyptus trees in Australia are grown for their oil. There are three grades of eucalyptus oil: medicinal, which contains the compound eucalyptol (also called ci- neol); industrial, in which a component of the oil is used in mining operations; and aromatic, which is used in per- fumes and fragrant soap products
