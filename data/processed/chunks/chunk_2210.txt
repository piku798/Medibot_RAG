Illite clays have been used successfully by envi- ronmental managers to remove such heavy metals as lead, cadmium, and chromium from industrial wastewater. French green clay takes its name from the fact that rock quarries located in southern France enjoyed a virtual monopoly on its production until similar deposits of illite clays were identified in China, Montana, and Wyoming
