Bupleurum, or Chinese thoroughwax, is an Asian plant that has been used in traditional Chinese medi- cine and Japanese Kampo formulations to treat jock itch and other fungal skin infections. Bupleurum contains compounds known as saikosaponins, which have anti-al- lergic and anti-inflammatory effects
