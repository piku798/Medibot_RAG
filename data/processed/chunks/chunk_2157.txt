The upper leaves of the stem are more dangerous than the lower leaves. Foxglove is most toxic just before the seeds ripen. It tastes spicy hot or bitter and smells slightly bad. In folk medicine, foxglove was first used in Ireland. Its use spread to Scotland, England, and then to central Europe
