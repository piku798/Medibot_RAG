Paula Ford-Martin Fennel Description Fennel (Foeniculum vulgare), also known as F. offic- inale, is a member of the Umbelliferae (Apiaceae) or carrot family, along with dill (Anethum graveolens), car- away (Carum carvi), and anise (Pimpinella anisum). Fennel has a thick, spindle-shaped taproot that produces a pithy, smooth or finely-fluted round stem that may reach to 6 ft (1.8 m) in height
