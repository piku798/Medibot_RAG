. Dry mouth, together with dry eyes, is a core symp- tom of Sjögren’s syndrome, named for the Swedish physician who first described it. Sjögren’s syndrome is an autoimmune disorder in which the body’s white cells attack the glands that produce saliva and tears. It is a common cause of dry mouth in the elderly. Although psychiatric disorders involving dry mouth are unusual, several cases have been reported of somato- form disorders in which dry mouth is a central symptom
