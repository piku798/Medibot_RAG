Clare Hanrahan Gonorrhea Definition Gonorrhea is a highly contagious sexually transmit- ted disease (STD) caused by the Neisseria gonorrhoeae bacterium. The genitourinary tract is the main system that is usually affected, but gonorrhea can also spread to the rectum, the throat, and the eyes
