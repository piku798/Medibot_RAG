Digestive tract—The long tubular structure that handles all digestion, and the structures that con- nect to it, including the mouth, esophagus, stom- ach, and intestines. Goiter—An enlarged thyroid gland (a hormone- producing gland at the front of the neck). Goiters usually appear as obvious swelling in the neck
