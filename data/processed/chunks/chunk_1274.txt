Causes & symptoms Among men under age 35 who are sexually active, Chlamydia trachomatis or Neisseria gonorrhoeae are the most common causes of epididymitis. Nonsexually transmitted epididymitis is associated with urinary tract infections and is more common in men who have undergone surgery for urinary tract problems or who have anatomical abnormalities
