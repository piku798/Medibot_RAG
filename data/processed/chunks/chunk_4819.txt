Because hypertension can cause damage to the blood vessels in the eyes, the eyes may be checked with an instrument called an ophthalmoscope. The physician will look for thickening, narrowing, or hemorrhages in the blood vessels. A chest x ray can detect an enlarged heart, other heart abnormalities, or lung disease.
