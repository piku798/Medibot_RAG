In this way, true diarrhea is distinguished from diseases that cause only an increase in the number of bowel movements (hyper- defecation), or incontinence (involuntary loss of bowel contents). Diarrhea is also classified by physicians into acute, which lasts one to two weeks, and chronic, which contin- ues for longer than 23 weeks. Viral and bacterial infec- tions are the most common causes of acute diarrhea
