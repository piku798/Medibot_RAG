Bioterrorism—The intentional use of disease- causing microbes or other biologic agents to in- timidate or terrorize a civilian population for po- litical or military reasons. Type A influenza virus could be used as an agent of bioterrorism. Common cold—An illness caused by an upper respiratory viruses. Usual symptoms include nasal congestion, coughing, sneezing, throat irritation, and a low-grade fever
