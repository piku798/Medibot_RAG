. Skin infections Honeysuckle works well against internal infections, and it can also be used externally for skin irritation and infections. Honeysuckle has been found useful in allevi- ating rashes ranging from skin diseases to poison oak. For these types of skin ailments, honeysuckle is best used as a poultice. For cuts and abrasions that may be- come infected, a honeysuckle infusion can be applied ex- ternally. It is in treating skin infections that the stems of honeysuckle are used
