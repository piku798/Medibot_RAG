. Description Ingrown nail (onychocryptosis) occurs when the nail plate (the horny covering) grows into and cuts the skin alongside the nail (lateral nail fold). Ingrown toenails make up 3–5% of all foot problems. Most cases of in- grown nail occur in men between the ages of 10–30 years. In this age group, males are affected twice as often as females.
