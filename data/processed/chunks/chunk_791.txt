. Koosh ball—A lightweight, “furry” ball of rubber band material used in Davis technique exercises for retraining neuropathways in the brain of a per- son with dyslexia. Mind’s eye—A term referring to an imaginary point from which the mind views what the eyes look at or what the imagination presents. In dyslexia, the mind’s eye is unanchored to one location, and sends many signals to the brain about what it sees, which causes disabling confusion
