Garlic (Allium sativum), myrrh (Commiphora molmol), oats (Avena sativa) may help reduce cholesterol and hawthorn (Crataegus spp.), linden (Tilia europaea), and yarrow (Achillea millefolium) are sometimes recommended to control high blood pressure, a risk factor for heart dis- ease. Tea, especially green tea (Camellia sinensis), is high in antioxidants; studies have shown that it may have a preventative effect against atherosclerosis
