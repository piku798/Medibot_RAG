. The term hyperthyroidism covers any disease that re- sults in an overabundance of thyroid hormone. Other names for hyperthyroidism, or specific diseases within the category, include Graves’ disease, diffuse toxic goiter, Basedow’s disease, Parry’s disease, and thyrotoxicosis. Hyperthyroidism affects 2.5 million people in the United States, but could affect up to 4.5 million people because more than half of the people with thyroid disease don’t know they have it
