. Prevention Preventing hives depends on avoiding the allergen causing them. Analysis of new items in the diet or new drugs taken may reveal the likely source of the reaction. Chronic hives may be aggravated by stress, caffeine, al- cohol, or tobacco; avoiding these may reduce the fre- quency of reactions.
