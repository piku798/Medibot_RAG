Various factors can increase stomach pressure, caus- ing gastroesophageal reflux: • obesity • lying down within one or two hours of eating • tight clothing • Pregnancy, which causes the enlarged uterus to displace the stomach, delaying the removal of stomach contents Eating too fast, chewing insufficiently, and smoking all increase stomach acid production. Smoking also dries up saliva that protects the esophagus from acid
