. The ends of each articular bone are covered by a layer of cartilage. Both the articular bones and the joint cavity are surrounded by a tough tissue called the articular cap- sule. The articular capsule has two components: the fi- brous membrane on the outside, and the synovial mem- brane (or synovium) on the inside. The fibrous mem- brane may include tough bands of fibrous tissue called ligaments, which are responsible for providing support to the joints
