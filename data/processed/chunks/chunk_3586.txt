Cochlea—A snail-shaped structure inside the inner ear, which contains the organ of Corti as well as fluid-filled compartments through which sound waves travel. Decibel—A unit of the intensity of sound, a mea- sure of loudness. Meniere’s disease—The combination of vertigo and decreased hearing caused by abnormalities in the inner ear
