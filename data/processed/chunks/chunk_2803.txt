. For each serving of tea, two or more teaspoons of ginseng root are recommended. Ginseng root can also be made into a tincture using alcohol, as ginsenocides are soluble and well-preserved in alcohol. Vodka or clear alcohol can be used, and the ginseng roots should be chopped finely or put in a blender with the alcohol. Enough alcohol should be used to completely saturate and cover the roots, and the solu- tion should be kept in a sealed glass bottle for a month or longer
