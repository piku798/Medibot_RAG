Alopecia areata and alopecia circumscripta refer to hair loss conditions that can be patchy or extend to com- plete baldness. The exact cause of alopecia areata is un- known, but it is thought to be triggered by an immune system disorder. Oftentimes, conditions affecting the skin of the scalp will result in hair loss
