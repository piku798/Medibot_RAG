. There are two main categories of depression: major depressive disorder and dysthymic disorder. Major de- pressive disorder is a moderate to severe episode of de- pression lasting two or more weeks. Individuals experi- encing this major depressive episode may have trouble sleeping, lose interest in activities in which they once took pleasure, experience a change in weight, have diffi- culty concentrating, feel worthless and hopeless, or have a preoccupation with death or suicide
