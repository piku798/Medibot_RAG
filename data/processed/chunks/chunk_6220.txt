Diagnosis Kidney infections can be diagnosed by family doc- tors, OB/GYN doctors, and urologists (doctors who spe- cialize in the urinary system). The diagnosis of kidney infection is based primarily on symptoms, urinalysis, and urine cultures. Blood tests may also be performed.
