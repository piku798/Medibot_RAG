. (JLM Visuals. Reproduced by permission.) General use Eucalyptus is most popular for its ability to clear congestion due to colds, coughs, flu, asthma, and si- nusitis. The tannins found in eucalyptus have astringent properties that reduce mucous membrane inflammation of the upper respiratory tract. Eucalyptol, the chemical component of the oil, works to loosen phlegm. Cough drops containing eucalyptus promote saliva production, which increases swallowing and lessens the coughing impulse
