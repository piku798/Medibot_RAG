For up to 70% of people, a primary infection causes general symptoms such as tiredness, headache, fever, chills, muscle aches, loss of appetite, and painful, swollen lymph nodes. These symp- toms are greatest during the first three to four days of the infection and disappear within a week. Most people with genital herpes experience pro- dromes, or symptoms of the oncoming disease
