The flavor of kola nut in cola beverages appears to be more effective than other flavors in disguising the taste of activated charcoal when the charcoal must be given as an antidote for accidental poisoning. Preparations The part of the seed known as the kola nut is the cotyledon, which is also called the seed leaf. The cotyle- dons are white and bitter when they are fresh, but they turn reddish with almost no taste when they are dried
