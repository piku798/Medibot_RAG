. The word gin comes from the Dutch word for juniper, “geniver.” In addition to being an ingredient in alcohol, juniper also has medic- inal properties. Ancient Egyptian doctors used the oil as a laxative as far back as 1550 B.C. The Zuni Native American people used the berries to assist them in child- birth.
