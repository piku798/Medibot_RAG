. Description Heavy metals are chemical elements that have a spe- cific gravity (a measure of density) at least five times that of water. The heavy metals most often implicated in human poisoning are lead, mercury, arsenic, and cadmi- um. Some heavy metals, such as zinc, copper, chromi- um, iron, and manganese, are required by the body in small amounts, but can be toxic in larger quantities. Heavy metals may enter the body through food, water, or air, or by absorption through the skin
