. Tophus (plural, tophi)—A chalky deposit of a uric acid compound found in gout. Tophi occur most frequently around joints and in the external ear. Grains-of-paradise fruit Description Grains-of-paradise fruit is a member of the Zingiber- aceae family (ginger group), which is a major family of tropical and subtropical fruits. It is also known as Guinea grains, Melegueta pepper, Piper melegueta and Aframo- mum melegueta roscoe, which is its botanical name
