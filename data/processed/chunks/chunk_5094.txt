Various cancers respond differently to IAT: • Bladder cancer responds favorably. • Brain cancer: astrocytomas (noncapsulated brain tu- mors arising in brain cells called astrocytes), grades I and II, respond favorably; grade III does not respond well; glioblastoma multiforme (the fastest-growing type of brain tumor) is not a candidate for IAT. • Breast cancer: all types respond, although inflammato- ry cancers respond poorly
