Nutritional therapy To prevent dizziness, nutritionists often advise eat- ing smaller but more frequent meals and avoiding caf- feine, nicotine, alcohol, foods high in fat or sugar, or any substances that cause allergic reactions. A low-salt diet may also be helpful to some people. Nutritionists may also recommend certain dietary supplements: • Magnesium citrate, aspartate or maleate: for dizziness caused by magnesium deficiency
