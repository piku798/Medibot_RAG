A traditional Chinese herbal formula typically con- tains four classes of ingredients, arranged in a hierarchi- cal order: a chief (the principal ingredient, chosen for the patient’s specific illness); a deputy (to reinforce the chief’s action or treat a coexisting condition); an assis- tant (to counteract side effects of the first two ingredi- ents); and an envoy (to harmonize all the other ingredi- ents and convey them to the parts of the body that they are to treat)
