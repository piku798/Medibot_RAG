Risk factors for infections include chronic disease; severe emotional stress; broken skin; changes in the pH of various body fluids; malnutrition; surgery; rupture of amniotic membranes; invasive medical or dental proce- dures; tissue injuries or destruction; decreased flow of body fluids; changes in peristalsis; decreased output of stomach acid; recurrent use of antibiotics; and suppressed immune function. Many infections have a high probabili- ty of being passed from person to person
