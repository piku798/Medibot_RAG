Bodywork—A term that covers a variety of thera- pies that include massage, realignment of the body, and similar techniques to treat deeply in- grained stresses and traumas carried in the tissues of the body. Chronic—Referring to a disease or condition that progresses slowly but persists or reoccurs over time.
