. Description Guided imagery is simply the use of one’s imagina- tion to promote mental and physical health. It can be self-directed, where the individual puts himself into a re- laxed state and creates his own images, or directed by others. When directed by others, an individual listens to a therapist, video, or audiotaped exercise that leads him through a relaxation and imagery exercise.
