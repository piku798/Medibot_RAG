. Calming herbs are often useful for relief of pain and tension. Cups of chamomile (Matricaria recutitca), cat- nip (Nepeta cataria), or lemon balm (Melissa officinalis) tea can be given freely for a calming effect. Fifteen drops of skullcap (Scutellaria lateriflora), St. John’s wort (Hypericum perforatum), or valerian (Valeriana offici- nalis) tincture can be given every half hour as needed
