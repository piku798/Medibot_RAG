. Gerson therapy The Gerson therapy treatment is based on drinking freshly pressed vegetable and fruit juice every hour. Dur- ing a typical day at a Gerson clinic, a person would drink 13 glasses of raw carrot/apple and green-leaf vegetable juices. Vegetarian meals of organically grown food are served. During treatment, the patient receives caffeine enemas during the evening to detoxify the blood and tis- sues, according to the Gerson Institute website
