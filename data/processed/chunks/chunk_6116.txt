Traditionally the root was chewed, spit into a bowl, and mixed with coconut milk or water. That practice is no longer the standard. Captain James Cook has been credited with the Western discovery of kava kava during his journey to the South Pacific in the late 1700s. The first herbal products made from kava kava appeared in Europe in the 1860s
