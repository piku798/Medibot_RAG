. Coronary arteries—The main arteries that provide blood to the heart. The coronary arteries surround the heart like a crown, coming out of the aorta, arching down over the top of the heart, and divid- ing into two branches. These are the arteries in which heart disease occurs. HDL cholesterol—High-density lipoprotein cho- lesterol is a component of cholesterol that helps protect against heart disease.
