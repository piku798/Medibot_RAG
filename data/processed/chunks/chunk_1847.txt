Flowers bloom in mid to late-summer in flat-topped clusters at the end of stems that may reach to a height of three feet. Smaller than daisies, and without the protruding central disk of chamomile, feverfew blossoms have yellow centers con- sisting of tightly-bunched tubular florets surrounded by creamy white rays. Bees seem to avoid feverfew, de- terred by its pungent aroma
