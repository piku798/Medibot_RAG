. . . . . . . . Analgesics—A class of pain-relieving medicines, including aspirin and acetominophen. Biofeedback—A technique in which a person is taught to consciously control the body’s response to a stimulus. Cephalalgia—The medical term for headache. Chronic—A condition that occurs frequently or continuously. Neurotransmitter—Any of a group of chemicals that transmit nerve impulses across the gap (synapse) between two nerve cells. Nociceptor—A specialized type of nerve cell that senses pain
