. Amino sugars are different from other body sugars, as they form part of carbohydrates. Their function is also different as they are not a source of energy, but rather are included in body tissue structure. Therefore, glucosamine plays a role in forming and maintaining the body’s tissues— for exam- ple, constructing nails, skin, eyes, bones, ligaments, ten- dons, heart valves, discharging mucus from the respira- tory system, digestive system, and urinary tract
