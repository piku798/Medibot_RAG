There is nor- mal variation in eyeball length and curvature of the lens and cornea, and some combinations of these variables give rise to eyes in which the cornea is too flat for the distance between the cornea and the retina. If the hyper- opia is not too severe, the lens may be able to accommo- date and bring the image back onto the retina. This acco- modation results in clear distance vision, but the constant focusing could cause headaches or eyestrain
