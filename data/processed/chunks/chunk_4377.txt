. Preparations Case-taking The first step in acute prescribing is a lengthy inter- view with the patient, known as case-taking. In addition to noting the character, location, and severity of the pa- tient’s symptoms, the homeopath will ask about their modalities. The modalities are the circumstances or fac- tors (e.g., weather, time of day, body position, behavior or activity, etc.) that make the symptoms either better or worse
