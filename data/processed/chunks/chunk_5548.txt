General use Treatment of poisoning Standard medical practice uses ipecac to cause vom- iting in cases of poisoning in order to remove the toxic substance from the stomach before absorption occurs. It can be used on animals as well as humans. Ipecac is safer and more effective than many other methods for in- ducing vomiting, such as sticking a finger down a child’s throat or using saltwater
