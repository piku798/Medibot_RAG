Herniated disk Definition Disk herniation is a breakdown of a fibrous cartilage material (annulus fibrosus) that makes up the interverte- bral disk. The annulus fibrosus surrounds a soft gel-like substance in the center of the disk called the nucleus pul- posus. Pressure from the vertebrae above and below may cause the nucleus pulposus to be forced against the sides of the annulus
