. Oral medications Oral medications are available to lower blood glucose in Type II diabetics. Drugs first prescribed for Type II dia- betes are in a class of compounds called sulfonylureas and include tolbutamide, tolazamide, acetohexamide, chlor- propamide, glyburide, glimeperide, and glipizide.
