. Homeopathic remedies There are several hundred homeopathic remedies. Homeopathic medicines are usually formulated from di- luted or triturated natural substances, including plants, minerals, or even venom from snakes or stinging insects. Some remedies may be given in a spray, ointment, or cream, but the most common forms of administration are liquid dilutions and two sizes of pellets, or cylindrical tablets (for triturated remedies)
