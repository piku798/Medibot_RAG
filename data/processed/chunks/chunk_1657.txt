A physician can rule out physical conditions and diseases that feature fa- tigue as a symptom, and can also determine if prescrip- tion drugs, poor dietary habits, work environment, or other external stressors could be triggering the exhaus- tion. Several diagnostic tests may also be required to rule out common physical causes of exhaustion, such as blood tests to check for iron-deficiency anemia. Diagnosis of chronic fatigue syndrome is signifi- cantly more difficult
