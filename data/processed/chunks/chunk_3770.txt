In severe cases of heavy metal poisoning, patients exhibit obvious impairment of cognitive, motor, and lan- guage skills. The expression “mad as a hatter” comes from the mercury poisoning prevalent in seventeenth-century France among hatmakers who soaked animal hides in a solution of mercuric nitrate to soften the hair
