To make a compress, 1-2 tablespoons of dried eye- bright are simmered in 1 pint (0.4 l) of water for 10 min- utes. The mixture is cooled and then strained. A clean cloth is dipped in the mixture, wrung out, and then placed over the eyes for 15 minutes several times daily. Caution should be used when applying compresses
