Possible side effects of too much T4 or T3 include osteoporosis (after long-term use), occasional anxiety, heart palpitations (very fast, strong heartbeat), insomnia, and occasional episodes of mania (acting crazed). Regular exercise and a high-fiber diet can help maintain thyroid function and prevent constipation.
