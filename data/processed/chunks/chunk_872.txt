. Nutrition Some practitioners believe that food allergies may in- crease the risk of ear infections, and they suggest eliminat- ing suspected food allergens from the diet. The top food allergens are wheat, dairy products, corn, peanuts, citrus fruits, and eggs. Elimination of sugar and sugar products can allow the immune system to work more effectively. Other nutritionists have noted that children who were breastfed as babies are less susceptible to ear infections
