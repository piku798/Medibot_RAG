Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)—A group of symp- toms, including nervous tension, irritability, tender breasts, and headache, experienced by some women in the days before menstruation caused by changes in hormone levels. Prostaglandin—An unsaturated fatty acid in humans that helps to control smooth muscle contraction, blood pressure, inflammation, and body temperature.
