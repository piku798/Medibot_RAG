. The three most common chelating agents are edetate calcium disodium, dimercaprol (BAL), and penicillamine. Succimer (DMSA) is used for children suffering from lead poison- ing. The chelating agent encircles and binds the metal in the body’s tissues, forming a complex that is then re- leased from the tissue and travels in the bloodstream. The complex is filtered out of the blood by the kidneys and excreted in the urine
