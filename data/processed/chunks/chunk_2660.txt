Prevention The only way to definitely prevent a genital herpes infection is to avoid contact with infected people. This is not an easy solution because many people aren’t aware that they are infected. Use of condoms and spermicidal jellies or foams with nonoxynol-9 is recommended with all partners whose disease status is questionable or un- known
