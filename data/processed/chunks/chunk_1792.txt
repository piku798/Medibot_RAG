. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Anticoagulant—Any substance that inhibits clot- ting action. Antioxidants—Enzymes that bind with free radi- cals to neutralize their harmful effects on living tissue. Galactogogue—A substance that stimulates the production of breast milk in nursing mothers
