. In the West, fritillaria is most commonly regarded as an ornamental garden plant. By contrast, it is tradi- tionally valued as an herbal remedy in Nepal and China, where it grows in the Gansu, Qinghai, Sichuan, Xizang, and Yunnan provinces. Two related species, F. thunbergii and F. hupehensis, are also used medicinally, and in some regions, F. unibracteata, F. przewalski, and F. delavayi are used as botanical substitutes
