Symptoms for those suffering environmental illness in- clude unexplained fatigue, increased allergies, hypersen- sitivity to common materials, intolerance to certain foods and indigestion, aches and pains, low-grade fever, headaches, insomnia, depression, sore throats, sudden weight loss or gain, lowered resistance to infection, gen- eral malaise, and disability.
