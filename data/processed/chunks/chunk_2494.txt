The types of garlic preparations include: • garlic oil capsules • encapsulated powdered garlic • odorless garlic pills • allicin-stabilized pills • aged garlic extract Used externally A poultice can be made using grated or crushed fresh garlic. The herb material should be placed directly on the site of injury or eruption, either “as is” or mixed with enough honey to make a paste. The poultice can be held in place with a cloth or bandage
