Many patients experience severe pain in the affected part during rewarming treatment and an intense throbbing pain that arises two or three days later and can last days or weeks. As the skin begins to thaw during treatment, edema often occurs, causing swelling in the area. In second- and higher-degree frostbite, blisters ap- pear.
