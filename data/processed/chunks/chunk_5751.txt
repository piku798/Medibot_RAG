There are several experimental surgical procedures: atherectomy, in which the surgeon shaves off and re- moves strips of plaque from the blocked artery; laser an- gioplasty, in which a catheter with a laser tip is inserted to burn or break down the plaque; and insertion of a metal coil, called a stent, that can be implanted perma- nently to keep a blocked artery open. This stenting pro- cedure is becoming more common
