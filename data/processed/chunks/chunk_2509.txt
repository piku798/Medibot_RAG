HDL—Beneficial lipoprotein molecules that trans- port cholesterol to the liver to be processed and excreted, thereby lowering cholesterol levels. LDL—Lipoproteins that transport cholesterol to body tissues for storage and thereby raise choles- terol levels. Plaque—A buildup of fats, cholesterol, calcium, and fibrous tissue in the blood that tends to attach to and weaken artery walls
