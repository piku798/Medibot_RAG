When the immune system be- comes alerted to the urate crystals, it mounts an inflam- matory response that includes the pain, redness, swelling, and damage to joint tissue that are the hall- marks of an acute gout attack. The body’s uric acid production tends to increase in males during puberty. Therefore, it should come as no surprise that nine out of ten of those suffering from gout are men
