Guggul Description The mukul myrrh tree, or Commiphora mukul, is small, thorny, and usually devoid of foliage. It grows natu- rally throughout India and Arabia. Guggul is the gum resin that comes from this tree, which belongs to the same genus as myrrh and has some similar components and actions
