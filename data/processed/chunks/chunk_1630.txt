. Many physiological changes occur in the body dur- ing fasting. During the first day or so, the body uses its glycogen reserves, the sugars that are the basic energy supply. After these are depleted, the body begins using fat. However, the brain, which has high fuel require- ments, still needs glucose (sugars converted from glyco- gen).
