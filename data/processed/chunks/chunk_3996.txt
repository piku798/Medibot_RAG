. The Middle Ages in Europe were a time of wide- spread death by plagues and pestilence. The Black Plague of 1348, particularly, and other health catastro- phes in later years, claimed so many lives that survivors began to lose faith in the dominant Galenic medical sys- tem. Fortunately, the knowledge of traditional herbal medicine had not been lost.
