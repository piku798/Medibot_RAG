. Ginsenocide—Active substances found in gin- seng. Ginseng, Siberian Description Siberian ginseng, Eleutherococcus senticosus, is also known as eleuthero ginseng or eleuthero. It is in the same botanical family as Korean ginseng (Panax gin- seng) and American ginseng (Panax quinquefolius). Siberian ginseng is one of the most widely used herbs in the world. Siberian ginseng is a thin, thorny shrub that grows up to 15 ft (4.6 m) high
