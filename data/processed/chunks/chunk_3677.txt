Research on an- tioxidants continues to send mixed messages, with some reports showing that vitamins E, C, and other antioxi- dants can help prevent heart disease, and other studies showing they have no effect. Many physicians and re- searchers therefore recommend that those wanting to fol- low healthy heart habits continue to eat a diet rich in an- tioxidants but recognize that there is probably no value in adding antioxidant supplements to a good diet
