. Native American plains Indians relied on echinacea as an all-purpose antiseptic. The Sioux tribe valued the root as a remedy for snake bite, the Cheyenne tribe chewed the root to quench thirst, and another tribe washed their hands in a decoction of echinacea to in- crease their tolerance of heat. European settlers learned of the North American herb’s many uses, and soon nu- merous echinacea-based remedies were commercially available from pharmaceutical companies in the United States
