Reported potential side effects of the stimulants include nervousness and insomnia, and are contra-indi- cated with epilepsy, allergies, blood pressure problems, or with use of monoamine oxidase (MAO) inhibitors. Long-term use of stimulants in children are reported to adversely affect growth, may ironically depress the ner- vous system or lead to loss of consciousness.
