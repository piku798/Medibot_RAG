Most Chinese herbalists use dong quai in combi- nation with other herbs depending on the problems being addressed and these are prepared together. Alcohol tincture: Combine fresh or dry, chopped root with enough alcohol to cover in a glass container. Alcohol should be of good quality. A 50/50 alcohol/water ratio is optimal. If the alcohol is not 100 proof, add pure water to obtain a 50/50 ratio
