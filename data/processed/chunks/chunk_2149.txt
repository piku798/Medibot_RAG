Other than informing the public, food poisoning prevention ef- forts include: • hot foods should be kept hot, and cold foods should be kept cold • meat should be cooked to the recommended internal temperature; eggs should be cooked until no longer runny • leftovers should be refrigerated promptly and food should never be left to stand at room temperature • contact of utensils and surfaces with the juices of raw meats should be avoided • fruits and vegetables should be washed before using •
