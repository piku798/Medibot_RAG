Causes & symptoms Hiccups can be caused by disorders of the central nervous system, by injury or irritation to the phrenic and vagus nerves, and by toxic or metabolic disorders affect- ing the central or peripheral nervous systems. They may be of unknown cause or may be a symptom of psycho- logical stress. Hiccups often occur after drinking car- bonated beverages or alcohol. They may also follow overeating or rapid temperature changes
