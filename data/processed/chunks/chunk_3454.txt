. Side effects Hawthorn rarely has side effects. In high doses, hawthorn can cause a severe drop in blood pressure, ar- rhythmias, and sedation. Interactions Since hawthorn performs the same function as some nitrates, cardiac glycosides, central nervous system de- pressants, and medications for high blood pressure, lower doses of these medications might be needed.
