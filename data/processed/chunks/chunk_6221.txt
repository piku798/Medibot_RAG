. Approximately 20% of patients have bacteria in the bloodstream, a condition called bacteremia. Urine dip- sticks that detect signs of infection are often used right in the doctor’s office. Urine would be examined with a mi- croscope for the presence of bacteria and leukocytes (white blood cells).
