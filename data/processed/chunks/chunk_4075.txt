. Treatment It is unclear if herniated disks cause pain themselves, or if they must press on a nerve root to cause pain. Pain may also occur with herniated disks as a result of me- chanical or neurological irritation of surrounding struc- tures such as muscles, tendons, ligaments, or joint cap- sules. Therefore, many treatment strategies will be pri- marily focused on managing symptoms that occur in con- junction with a herniated disk
