Peppermint is one of the oldest medicinal herbs. Peppermint has three major actions in the body: it re- duces nausea and vomiting, it encourages the liver to produce bile, and it clears the stomach of imbalanced bacteria. It is particularly useful for treating spastic colon, irritable bowel syndrome and diarrhea
