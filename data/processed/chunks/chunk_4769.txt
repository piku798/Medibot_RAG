Close work—Tasks that cause the eyes to focus on something close at hand, such as reading, writing, computer work, and sewing. Cornea—The clear, dome-shaped outer covering of the front of the eye. Iris—The colored ring of muscle that controls the amount of light allowed to reach the retina. Pupil—The black hole in the center of the iris through which light enters on the way to the lens and retina
