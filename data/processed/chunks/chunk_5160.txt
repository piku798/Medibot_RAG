. Prevention There is no specific treatment to prevent impotence. Perhaps the most important measure is to maintain gen- eral good health and avoid atherosclerosis by exercising regularly, controlling weight, controlling hypertension and high cholesterol levels, and not smoking. Avoiding excessive alcohol intake may also help. Resources BOOKS “Erectile Dysfunction.” Section 17, Chapter 220 in The Merck Manual of Diagnosis and Therapy, edited by Mark H.
