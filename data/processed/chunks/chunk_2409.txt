Treatment Chelation therapy is a treatment that uses an intra- venous solution containing the drug ethylenediamine tetra-acetic acid (EDTA), among other substances. In the bloodstream, EDTA binds and removes toxins and plaque formation on arterial walls. It promotes circula- tion throughout the body, and is reportedly, although not proven, able to reverse the processes leading to gan- grene
