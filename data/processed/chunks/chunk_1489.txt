. In Chinese medicine, the kidneys store jing. Jing is an essential life source and associated with whole body growth and de- velopment, as well as normal sexual and reproductive functioning. The kidney and liver jing also affects the bones, ligaments, and tendons. In the Chinese system of health, yin aspects must be kept in balance with yang aspects. Ill health occurs when the energies and elements of the body are out of balance or in disharmony
