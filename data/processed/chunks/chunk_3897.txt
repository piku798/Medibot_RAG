. Hepatitis G HGV, also called hepatitis GB virus, was first de- scribed in early 1996. Little is known about the frequen- cy of HGV infection, the nature of the illness, or how to prevent it. What is known is that transfused blood con- taining HGV has caused some cases of hepatitis. For this reason, patients with hemophilia and other bleeding con- ditions who require large amounts of blood or blood products are at risk of constructing hepatitis G
