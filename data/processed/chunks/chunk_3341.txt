If people diagnosed with Type I or Type II diabetes are taking insulin to con- trol their blood sugar, they cannot replace the insulin with gymnema. In addition, diabetes can go undetected for some time. It may not be diagnosed until a person goes to a doctor after experiencing symptoms such as frequent uri- nation, dizziness, and fatigue
