. Description Water can be used therapeutically in a number of ways. Common forms of hydrotherapy include: • Whirlpools, Jacuzzis, and hot tubs. These soaking tubs use jet streams to massage the body. They are frequent- ly used by physical therapists to help injured patients regain muscle strength and to soothe joint and muscle pain. Some midwives and obstetricians also approve of the use of hot tubs to soothe the pain of labor. • Pools and Hubbard tanks
