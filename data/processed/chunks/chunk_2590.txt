Typical ex- posure to these viruses occurs through the fecal-to-oral route, by ingesting food that is contaminated with fecal material or by coming in contact with an infected per- son’s vomit or diarrhea and then inadvertently bringing the contaminant to the mouth. Other routes of transmis- sion are quite likely, because exposure to as few as 100 virus particles can cause an infection. Typically, children are more vulnerable to rotavirus- es—the most common cause of acute watery diarrhea
