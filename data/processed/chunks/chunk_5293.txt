Ovulatory problems The first step in diagnosing ovulatory problems is to make sure that an ovum is being produced each month. A woman’s morning body temperature is slightly higher around the time of ovulation. A woman can measure and record her temperatures daily and a chart can be drawn to show whether or not ovulation has occurred. Luteiniz- ing hormone (LH) is released just before ovulation
