. Hyperuricemia—High levels of uric acid in the bloodstream. Kidney stones—Hard lumpy masses of mineral wastes that are formed in the kidneys and may cause blockages. Purine—A substance found in foods that is broken down into urate and may contribute to hyper- uricemia and gout. Synovial fluid—Fluid surrounding the joints which acts as a lubricant, reducing the friction between the joints. Tophus (plural, tophi)—A chalky deposit of a uric acid compound found in gout
