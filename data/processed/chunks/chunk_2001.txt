. Interactions Consumers should consult their healthcare profes- sional for information on flaxseed products and interac- tions with medications and other remedies. More specifi- cally, the omega-3 fatty acids in flaxseed may increase the blood-thinning effects of such medications as aspirin or warfarin. Flaxseed may help a group of medications known as statins (lovastatin, simvastatin, etc.), which are given to lower blood cholesterol, to work more effectively
