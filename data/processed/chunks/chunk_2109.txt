Almost any food can be contaminated, but salad dressings, milk products, cream pastries, and food kept at room tempera- ture, rather than hot or cold, are likely candidates. Clas- sic symptoms of food poisoning appear rapidly, usually two to eight hours after the contaminated food is eaten. Such symptoms usually last only three to six hours and rarely more than two days
