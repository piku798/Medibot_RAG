. Iodine tinctures (dilute mixtures of alcohol and iodine) or Betadine are used as antiseptics to kill bacteria in skin cuts. Atomi- dine (a product containing iodine trichloride and other unlisted ingredients) is also sold as an antiseptic. Atomi- dine taken orally in minute cyclic doses is also recom- mended as a glandular stimulant and purifier. Some research has shown that oral iodine supple- ments have antifibrotic and anti-inflammatory effects
